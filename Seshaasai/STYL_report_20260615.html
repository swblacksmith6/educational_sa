What Can Break the Thesis

  1. Top line keeps stalling — two straight years of revenue decline; if payments don't recover, margin expansion runs out of room.
  2. Customer concentration — top 10 ~66%, largest ~17%, ~84% BFSI, no exclusive/long-term volume contracts.
  3. Optionality stays optional — SIM/eSIM & metal-card RFPs early and un-guided; only proven lever (margins) is finite.

Final Word

One HIGH trigger (margin/mix re-rating, already delivered) plus a cluster of MEDIUM triggers (IoT +45%, de-levered balance sheet, tender pipeline, SIM/eSIM certified-and-live) support a quality-compounder-at-a-reset-price thesis at ~17–18x FY26 earnings, 27%+ EBITDA margin, ~32% RoCE, zero promoter pledge. It does not yet support a high-growth re-rating — the biggest swing factor (payments volume, ~50% of revenue) is pure optionality. Watch for two consecutive quarters of double-digit YoY revenue growth and NWC days back below 80.
