### Q4 / FY26 (call 19 May 2026)

| Metric | Q4 FY26 | FY26 (full year) |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | ~₹405 cr (+8.1% QoQ, **+9.6% YoY**) | ₹1,441 cr (**−1.5% YoY**) |
| EBITDA | ~₹125 cr, **30.8%** (+330 bps YoY) | ₹394 cr, **27.4%** (+204 bps YoY) |
| PAT | ~₹82 cr, 20.2% (+316 bps YoY) | ₹240 cr, **16.7%** (+146 bps YoY) |

**Segment mix FY26:** Payments ~50% (47.5% in Q4), Comms 39% (**+29% YoY**), IoT 11% (**+45% YoY**). ~97–98% recurring revenue.

**New announcements:** Metal-card **patent granted**; first Indian card-maker to file (Oct 2021) & first with global scheme approvals (Mar 2023). Bengaluru facility received **GSMA SAS-UP certification** for SIM/eSIM — SIM division "started contributing materially to revenue this quarter"; working with a leading telecom player. 6 new patents filed in FY26 → 19 total applications (5 granted). 21 new payment customers added.

**Guidance:** On payments — *"as banking cycles normalize, we expect gradual improvement in volumes going forward"* (**MEDIUM**) and expects payments to *"bottom out and from here on... better momentum"* (**MEDIUM**). On FY27, management **declined formal guidance**: *"...maybe in a position to give a guidance after Q1. That's our take for now."* (no number — appropriately cautious, but a gap for modelling).

**Q4 key takeaway:** Strongest quarter of the year delivered on the promised H2 seasonal recovery, with record 30.8% EBITDA margin and 20%+ PAT margin; full-year top line still slightly negative, so the FY26 story is margin- and mix-led, not volume-led.

Execution on margins, IoT, SIM/eSIM, deleveraging and the seasonal H2 call was credible and on-time. The two soft spots are exactly the ones that matter for the *growth* thesis: **payments volume recovery has been slower than the "transient" framing implied, and big tenders are slow to convert to revenue.** No pattern of hollow optimism — management was generally measured (used MEDIUM, not HIGH, conviction for forward claims) and refused to over-promise FY27 guidance.

## Confidence Language Audit & Dodges

- Forward claims are consistently **MEDIUM conviction** ("we expect", "we are confident", "gradual improvement", "bottom out") rather than HIGH — appropriately humble, but means little is firmly committed.
- **Not answered / deferred:** FY27 growth guidance explicitly deferred to "after Q1"; metal-card RFP timelines left open ("still under evaluation"); SIM/eSIM revenue scale "not in a position to give guidance… from this low base."
- **Tone shift across the three calls:** improving — from defensive ("transient" stress, −12% H1) in Q2, to constructive (YoY growth resumes) in Q3, to confident (record margins, new SIM/metal optionality) in Q4. The arc is positive but the company is asking investors to trust optionality (IoT, SIM, metal cards) that is not yet material.

**Key takeaway (2 sentences):** Seshaasai's management delivered on the bankable promises — H2 seasonal recovery, margin expansion to a record 30.8% in Q4, IoT +45%, deleveraging — earning a MEDIUM–HIGH credibility read over its first three quarters as a listed company. The unfinished business is top-line growth: payments volumes are only "bottoming," large tenders haven't converted, and the genuine growth optionality (SIM/eSIM, metal cards) remains early and un-guided.
