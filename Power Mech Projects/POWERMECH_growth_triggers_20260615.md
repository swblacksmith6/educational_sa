# Power Mech Projects — Growth Triggers 1-Pager
*As of 15 Jun 2026 | CMP: ₹~2,780 | Mcap: ₹~7,840 cr | TTM P/E ~24x | Source: Screener.in + concalls/IP*

## Business in one line
India's largest power O&M services provider and a diversified E&C contractor (boiler/turbine/generator erection & commissioning, civil/EPC, and Balance-of-Plant) that has pivoted into long-life, high-margin contract mining (MDO) for coal and is scaling annuity O&M alongside a record power-capex order book.

## The Growth Triggers (5-7)

### Trigger 1: Two signed MDO coal-mining contracts (Tasra + KBP) ramping to peak — the structural margin/annuity engine [HIGH]
- What: Two long-dated Mine-Developer-Operator contracts — Tasra coking coal (SAIL) ₹30,383 cr over 28 yrs and Kotre Basantpur Pachmo / KBP (CCL/Coal India) ₹9,294 cr over 25 yrs — both now operational, ramping production toward peak (KBP 5 MT by FY30, Tasra 4 MT by FY29) at structurally higher EBITDA margins than legacy contracting.
- Evidence: "KBP mine, client is lifting the material beyond the contract capacity. INR 350 crores we are projecting for the FY27 and INR 500 crores for FY28 from KBP... Tasra... by mid of Q4 FY27 we'll start our production... INR 150 crores in FY27, and it will increase to INR 750 crores by FY28. MDO mix will be 7% in overall revenue for FY27... jump to 13% in FY28" (Q4 FY26 call). Contract values per Aug-25 IP slide 25: "Include MDO contract of Rs. 9,294 crores... Rs. 30,383 crores."
- Quantum: MDO revenue ~₹354 cr FY26 → ~₹500 cr FY27 → ~₹1,250 cr FY28; at full capacity (FY29/30) ~₹2,100–2,200 cr/yr. Blended MDO EBITDA 15% now → ~20-21% at peak (~+1% margin/yr). Drives group EBITDA margin from 12.3% toward 14%+ by FY30.
- Timeline: Revenue inflection FY27-FY28; peak rate capacity FY29 (Tasra/SAIL) and FY30 (KBP).

### Trigger 2: Record power-capex order book & inflow guidance ramp (₹55,151 cr backlog; FY27 target ₹12,000 cr) [HIGH]
- What: Total order backlog (incl. MDO) of ₹55,151 cr; executable book ex-MDO ₹15,899 cr (~2.5 yrs revenue). Thermal capex supercycle (Adani, NTPC, JSW, BHEL) underpins a step-up in inflow guidance from FY26's ₹7,210 cr to a ₹12,000 cr FY27 target.
- Evidence: "the total order backlog (including MDO) as on 31st March 2026 was Rs. 55,151 crore... Excluding MDO order, the executable book stands at around INR 15,899 crores... Looking ahead to FY27, the company is targeting order inflow of around INR 12,000 crores" (Q4 FY26 call); identified opportunity pipeline "about INR 70,000 crores" with ~15% hit ratio (Q4 FY26 call).
- Quantum: Backlog supports ~21% revenue growth guidance for FY27 (management); ₹6,062 cr FY26 → ~₹7,300 cr+ FY27. Power-sector item-rate + BOP opportunity pool sized at "plus INR 60,000 crore."
- Timeline: FY27-FY30 ("clear visibility of growth till FY30").

### Trigger 3: Entry into Balance-of-Plant (BOP) EPC — scope/margin up-shift [HIGH]
- What: First integrated BOP EPC win — 1×800 MW Singareni STPP from BHEL (~₹2,550 cr), moving Power Mech up the value chain from execution sub-packages to integrated EPC delivery, with ~40% in-house value-add leveraging existing civil/mechanical/commissioning strengths.
- Evidence: "the award of a large BOP EPC package for the 800-megawatt Singareni Thermal project from BHEL. This project marks an important milestone in expanding our capabilities from the execution packages to integrated EPC delivery" (Q4 FY26 call). Breakup (Q3 FY26 call): "mechanical is INR 1,550 crore, civil is about INR 720 crore, electrical is INR 280 crore," 38-month schedule, 10% advance, 5% retention.
- Quantum: ₹2,550 cr order; billing started Feb-26. Targeting 1-2 more BOP wins in FY27 from a pipeline of "two-three projects... worth INR 15,000 crores."
- Timeline: Execution FY26-FY29; incremental BOP wins FY27.

### Trigger 4: O&M expansion — largest player, annuity recurring revenue [HIGH]
- What: India's largest power O&M provider (~20% market share, 36.25 GW under O&M/AMC, ~18,000 skilled O&M staff). New unit commissioning of 8,000-10,000 MW/yr feeds a steady stream of long-tenure, high-margin (15-16%) annuity contracts; recent wins from SJVN, Vedanta, GMDC, Hindustan Zinc.
- Evidence: "O&M continues to be an interesting opportunity for us, and that is a margin driven... about 4,100 MW of ordering has been taken up in the O&M in the year 2025-26" (Q4 FY26 call); "add-on opportunity of about INR 1,200 crores to INR 1,500 crores, & that will be... every year added" (Q3 FY26 call). Active discussions: BALCO ~₹1,500 cr, Butibori ~₹700 cr.
- Quantum: O&M EBITDA 15-16% (vs <10% for construction); ~₹1,200-1,500 cr incremental O&M opportunity per year; O&M backlog ~₹2,500 cr.
- Timeline: Recurring FY27 onward.

### Trigger 5: FGD / emission-norm erection orders [OPTIONALITY]
- What: FGD/SCR emission-equipment erection was once a large pipeline, but regulatory deferral collapsed it — Power Mech already reclassified ₹4,264 cr of FGD orders out of the book as non-moving; only ~₹936 cr (Udupi) remains active. A re-acceleration would be upside but management is not banking on it.
- Evidence: "we have reclassified nonmoving category to the extent of INR 4,264 crores worth of FGD packages, and we removed it from the order book" (Q1 FY26 call); "Investment is going to come down with the recent Supreme Court order" (Q1 FY26 call); "Only INR 936 crore of Udupi project only running in the FGD" (Q3 FY26 call).
- Quantum: ~₹936 cr live; up to ~₹4,000+ cr latent if norms are re-enforced (not in guidance).
- Timeline: Indeterminate — policy dependent.

### Trigger 6: Metro / urban-mobility O&M + new EPC verticals (mine-handling, steel, nuclear, railways) [MEDIUM]
- What: New O&M vertical — entered metro O&M via Mumbai Monorail/Light Metro (₹279 cr), opening a large outsourcing pool as metro route-km doubles (1,095 → 2,000 km). Plus adjacent EPC optionality: NMDC mine-handling EPC (₹8,000-10,000 cr pipeline with Thyssenkrupp), steel-plant works, nuclear civil (₹563 cr Kaiga 2×700 MW), and railway electrification+signalling packages.
- Evidence: "a major breakthrough has been in entering into O&M business in the Metro sector, INR 279 crores... it can be a huge opportunity... for the changeover from the present system... to outsourcing by the metro companies" (Q4 FY26 call); NMDC: "Couple of projects... worth about INR 8,000 crores to INR 10,000 crores" (Q4 FY26 call).
- Quantum: Metro O&M ₹279 cr won (L1 on Bangalore Metro + Monorail per Q4 call); NMDC EPC ₹8,000-10,000 cr pipeline (bidding stage, not won).
- Timeline: Metro O&M FY27; EPC adjacencies FY27-FY28 (bid-dependent).

### Trigger 7: Green energy / BESS via PM Green [OPTIONALITY]
- What: Standalone utility-scale battery storage was positioned as a future growth engine via 100% subsidiary PM Green, but the flagship win was annulled — WBSEDCL cancelled the 250 MW/1,000 MWh BESS Letter of Award in Mar-26, cutting the order book by ₹1,563 cr and causing the FY26 inflow miss (₹7,210 cr vs ₹10,000 cr target). Treat as optionality only.
- Evidence: "the unexpected cancellation of the BESS order of Rs. 1,563 crore by WBSEDCL... total order inflow of Rs. 7,210 crore and was lower than expectations" (May-26 IP commentary); "there was an issue with the West Bengal Electricity Board and then it had to be cancelled. Otherwise, our numbers would have been much better" (Q4 FY26 call). Re-tender possible: "they will again be recalling the tender... this year, we may look at that order" (Q4 FY26 call).
- Quantum: ₹1,563 cr cancelled (₹103 cr/yr × 15 yrs, ~₹800 cr capex, 16-18% IRR target). Zero committed today; small 16 MW Bihar solar is the only live green asset.
- Timeline: Uncertain — depends on WBSEDCL re-tender.

## What can break the thesis (top 3 risks)
1. **MDO execution/ramp risk:** Revenue and margin uplift hinge on production ramp at Tasra (washery commissioning Dec-26, first washed coal Q4 FY27) and client offtake at KBP. Any washery delay, monsoon disruption, or slower-than-guided offtake pushes the margin inflection right (guidance already cut from ~₹1,800-1,900 cr FY28 in Feb-26 to ₹1,250 cr in May-26).
2. **Order-inflow / thermal-capex concentration:** FY26 inflow missed (₹7,210 cr vs ₹10,000 cr target) and the ₹12,000 cr FY27 goal leans heavily on Adani, BHEL, JSW thermal ordering and a ~15% hit ratio on a ₹70,000 cr pipeline; BHEL's changed packaging philosophy and price competition could compress wins and margins.
3. **Working-capital / receivable & low core margins:** Core construction EBITDA is <8-10%; the Water (JJM/UP) division is stalled on fund/certification delays (~₹700-900 cr stuck, ₹90 cr receivable + ₹128 cr uncertified), and a contractor-labour business carries chronic WC and labour-code cost pressure. New-vertical setbacks (BESS cancellation) show optionality can reverse.

## Verdict
The investment case stands on four HIGH-conviction, signed/contracted triggers — the two long-life MDO mining contracts (₹39,677 cr lifetime, now operational and ramping), a record ₹55,151 cr order book with credible ~21% revenue and ₹12,000 cr inflow guidance, the new BOP EPC vertical (₹2,550 cr BHEL order already billing), and a market-leading annuity O&M franchise. These alone justify the thesis and drive the margin mix from ~12.3% toward 14%+ by FY30 without needing the optional legs. FGD re-acceleration and BESS/green are pure upside — the latter notably impaired after WBSEDCL annulled the ₹1,563 cr BESS award in Mar-26 — and metro O&M/EPC adjacencies (MEDIUM) add a real but bid-dependent second growth wave.
