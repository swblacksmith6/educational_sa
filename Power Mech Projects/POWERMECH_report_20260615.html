⚖️ The central tension. The books are clean (unqualified audits, no pledge, trivial contingents, credible board) and the franchise is real — yet the Walk-the-Talk audit shows management missed its order-inflow target three years running and cut revenue guidance every year, often in high-conviction language. Good operators, unreliable forecasters. Underwrite the delivered items (O&M, execution capacity, cash recovery in FY26), discount the promised ones (far-dated MDO peak, ₹12,000 cr inflow).
