QoQ: Q3 FY26 PAT was Rs 100 cr; Q4 jumped to Rs 153 cr — the usual Q4 execution surge. Q4 EBITDA margin (11.17%) was the **weakest of the year**, management attributing it to "increase in the operating cost and the lower other income during the quarter."

### 2. Segment performance (Q4 FY26 vs Q4 FY25, where given for FY26 full year)
- **Mechanical/ETC + EPC**: strong — driven by ramp-up of Udupi FGD (Adani instructed completion by March) and the new Singareni BOP EPC.
- **O&M**: the standout — FY26 backlog grew ~8%; O&M is the margin engine (15–16% EBITDA). New wins: Mumbai Monorail O&M (Rs 279 cr, first metro O&M), Hindustan Zinc, BALCO (under discussion).
- **Civil**: largest backlog segment (~Rs 9,100 cr) but FY26 revenue muted by water-division stall.
- **Mining (MDO)**: FY26 MDO revenue ~Rs 354 cr (KBP Rs 248 cr over 5 months from Nov-25; Tasra Rs 106 cr). March alone did Rs 117 cr from KBP (exceptional, not sustainable run-rate).
- **Water (JJM)**: continued drag; ~Rs 700 cr of planned FY26 revenue not recognised due to fund/certification delays.
- **International**: ~4–5% of revenue, now purely O&M/manpower (ETC exited). FY26 overseas revenue Rs 327 cr vs Rs 292 cr FY25.

### 3. Demand commentary
Management "still very bullish." Power-sector ordering on BHEL+L&T over ~2 years cited at ~Rs 2.8 lakh cr / ~54,740 MW; PMPL opportunity from this ~Rs 60,000 cr in ETC/civil/structural/BOP. Government coal-capacity plan raised: "80 GW can go up to nearly 100 GW in the coal-based power plants." New verticals being chased: NMDC iron-ore (Rs 70,000 cr capex, ~Rs 10,000 cr opportunity), steel (SAIL/ArcelorMittal/JSW; ~Rs 10 lakh cr over 5–6 yrs), metro O&M, coal gasification (early), nuclear (Kaiga).

### 4. Margin drivers / pressures
- **Drivers**: rising O&M mix (15–16% EBITDA), MDO ramp (target ~20% at PRC), EPC value-add (~40% in-house on Singareni BOP).
- **Pressures**: water-division fixed overheads with no revenue; commodity inflation (steel/cement/diesel) on domestic civil/EPC — "to the extent PVC is covered... we'll get reimbursement"; one-time Labor Code provision of ~Rs 4.5 cr in Q4.
- Guidance: FY27 EBITDA "to touch 12.5%... increase of 0.25% to 0.3%." MDO PRC margins pushed to FY29/FY30.

### 5. Capex & balance sheet
- Gross debt Rs 622 cr, **net debt Rs 163 cr** as on 31-Mar-26 (down from Rs 753 cr gross / Rs 239 cr net at Q1 FY26). Debt/equity 0.32x.
- Operating cash flow turned strongly positive: **Rs 430 cr in FY26 vs Rs 0.74 cr (Rs 74 lakh) in FY25** — driven by receivables realisation.
- Capex FY27 guide: ~Rs 400 cr at PMPL level (mostly Tasra washery, on internal accruals "without any debt") + Rs 400 cr at Tasra SPV (coal handling plant + railway siding). Washery total ~Rs 690–700 cr.

### 6. Guidance (verbatim)
- Revenue FY27: "we are projecting around 21% growth" (Aravind); target revenue not explicitly stated as a Rs figure for FY27, but FY28 order-inflow "same range, the INR 12,000 crores to INR 15,000 crores range."
- Order inflow FY27: "the company is targeting order inflow of around INR 12,000 crores."
- EBITDA: "we are expecting to touch EBITDA of 12.5%, including other income. There may be a increase of 0.25% to 0.3% in the EBITDA."
- MDO: "INR 350 crores we are projecting for the FY27 and INR 500 crores for FY28 from KBP... Tasra... INR 150 crores revenue in FY27, and it will increase to INR 750 crores by FY28." Peak (FY29/FY30): "Around INR 2,100 crores to INR 2,200 crores."
- "There's a clear visibility of growth till FY'30."

### 7. New announcements / orders
- FY26 order inflow Rs 7,210 cr vs Rs 10,000 cr target (72% achieved).
- Singareni 800 MW BOP EPC from BHEL — Rs 2,550 cr (mech 1,550 / civil 720 / electrical 280), 38-month schedule, 10% advance, 5% retention.
- Mumbai Monorail O&M — Rs 279 cr (first metro O&M).
- **WBSEDCL cancelled the 250 MW / 1000 MWh BESS order (Rs 1,563 cr)** — see Part 2.
- Adani thermal (Mahan 2&3, Mirzapur 1, Raipur — 6,400 MW, ~Rs 3,166 cr ongoing); Kaiga nuclear civil Rs 563 cr.

### 8. Top-3 sharp analyst Q&A
1. **Mohit Kumar (ICICI):** "we guided for INR 10,000 crores order inflow. And I think there is a substantial miss." Management: shortfall "primarily attributable to the cancellation of battery energy storage system order worth of INR 1,563 crores."
2. **Rohan Advant (Prad Capital):** flagged that the Feb-26 call said FY28 MDO could be Rs 1,800–1,900 cr but May-26 says Rs 1,250 cr — "where's the gap now?" Management conceded conservative re-projection on washery/ramp risk; Q4's Rs 226 cr MDO is "not the sustainable monthly run rate."
3. **Tushar Khandelwal (Nexus):** "I see a lot of challenges in the MDO segment... what is the underlying change... from which we are seeming this confident?" Management: O&M high-margin focus + EPC ramp + MDO production ramp underpin the 21% FY27 growth claim.

### 9. Key takeaway (2 sentences)
Power Mech delivered a record FY26 (revenue Rs 6,107 cr, PAT Rs 412 cr) with materially improved cash flow (OCF Rs 430 cr) and net debt down to Rs 163 cr, but for the **third consecutive year missed its headline order-inflow target** (Rs 7,210 cr vs Rs 10,000 cr), worsened by the embarrassing WBSEDCL BESS cancellation that the company had been promoting as a growth engine just one quarter earlier. Execution and balance-sheet discipline are genuinely good; the chronic gap is between management's forward "opportunity-size" rhetoric (Rs 60,000–1,40,000 cr "tracked") and what actually converts.

### Management Credibility Rating: **LOW–MEDIUM (net −9; rated LOW on guidance, MEDIUM on execution/balance sheet)**

The honest verdict is **LOW** on forward guidance credibility and **MEDIUM** on operational delivery — a split personality. The aggregate −9 is dominated by a clear, repeated pattern of optimistic targets that were serially cut and missed. What rescues the company from an outright "LOW everything" is that the *delivered* items (#15, #16, #18 + recovering cash flow/debt) are exactly the things that protect downside: O&M growth, execution capacity, conversion discipline, and a balance sheet that never blew out.

### Explicit patterns of optimism-without-delivery (flagged aggressively)
1. **Revenue guidance cut every single year.** FY25: 30–35% → 25% actual. FY26: Rs 7,500 cr → Rs 6,500 cr → Rs 6,107 cr actual. The "30–35%" Feb-24 number was never close to being achieved.
2. **Order-inflow target missed three years running.** FY24 Rs 8,759 cr vs Rs 10,000 cr target; FY25 Rs 6,437 cr vs Rs 12,000 cr; FY26 Rs 7,210 cr vs Rs 10,000 cr. Management leans on giant "opportunity-size" numbers (Rs 60k–1,40k cr "tracked") that systematically over-promise conversion.
3. **"Working capital will normalise on MDO stabilisation" repeated for ~8 quarters** (Feb-24 through FY26) while net current days actually deteriorated; the real fix (late-FY26) came from water receivables collection, not MDO.
4. **MDO ramp and peak-revenue timelines slipped ~1.5–2 years** on every milestone (first coal, peak capacity, washery, FY28 revenue). The MDO thesis — the core long-term margin story — has been the least reliable.
5. **BESS over-promised then evaporated.** Touted as a growth engine in Feb-26; the WBSEDCL Rs 1,563 cr order was annulled in Mar-26, single-handedly causing the FY26 inflow miss. Management's "cautiously optimistic" framing in Feb-26 looks, in hindsight, like premature promotion.
6. **FGD: a year of "it stands" reassurance ended in Rs 4,264 cr of orders being quietly removed** from the book — a textbook case of defending a dead order until forced to de-recognise it.

## PART 4 — Tone shift across calls (FY24 → FY26)

Across the ten calls the **headline tone stayed relentlessly bullish** — "bullish," "opportunity size," "huge investments," and ever-larger tracked-pipeline numbers (Rs 15,000 cr in Feb-24 escalating to Rs 1,30,000–1,40,000 cr by Feb-26) recur in every transcript, largely unchanged in confidence even as actual order inflow and revenue repeatedly undershot. What **did** shift was the *substance beneath the optimism*: through FY24–FY25 the narrative leaned heavily on FGD, water (JJM) and aggressive MDO ramp timelines, all of which deteriorated (FGD de-recognised, water stalled on fund delays, MDO slipped ~2 years) — so the storyline visibly **rotated toward O&M, BOP EPC and new diversifications** (nuclear, BESS, metro O&M, steel/NMDC mining) as the older drivers disappointed. By the latest calls (Feb-26 / May-26) the tone became subtly **more defensive and caveated** — the May-26 call uniquely added macro-risk language ("global macroeconomic environment continues to remain volatile... supply shortages, inflationary pressures") and openly conceded the BESS cancellation and serial order-inflow misses, while the BESS episode forced a rare admission that a touted "growth engine" had evaporated. Net: management's *confidence in tone* never dimmed, but the *credibility of the specific promises* eroded steadily, and the most recent commentary shows the first genuine notes of humility (conservative MDO re-projection, hedged FY27 numbers) after three years of cut-and-miss.
