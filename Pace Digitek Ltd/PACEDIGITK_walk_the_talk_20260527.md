## Section 3 — Demand Commentary (across calls)

- **BESS demand is surging** — central PSUs (SECI, NTPC, NLC, DVC), state DISCOMs (MSEDCL, KPTCL, KREDL) all tendering aggressively; **government tightened local-content rules in 2026 Budget and removed capital-equipment duties for BESS manufacturing** (Feb-26 call, Venugopal Rao Maddisetty).
- **BSNL 4G saturation tower deployment complete**, now in 5-yr O&M phase (Q4 IP) — declining capex revenue but recurring O&M revenue stream begins.
- **Tariff compression concern:** Recent Rajasthan/Gujarat BESS tenders bid at ₹1.7 lakh/MW/month vs Pace's quoted band ₹2.19–2.55 lakh. Management expects "correction" as winners struggle to execute. (Nov-25 + Feb-26 calls.)
- **Export pivot starting** — Africa (Kenya) and Middle East for BESS to lift blended margins (Feb-26 call).

## Section 8 — Analyst Q&A — sharpest exchanges

1. **Dinesh (individual investor, Nov-25)** — Stripped down the BOO economics: "₹500 cr revenue × 85% EBITDA = ₹425 cr; minus depreciation ₹200 cr = ₹225 cr; minus interest on 70% debt of ₹2,200 cr × 9% = ₹200 cr → margin becomes near zero." **CFO conceded:** PAT will be only ₹50 cr on ₹500 cr revenue → effective BOO PAT margin **~10%, not the headline 85% EBITDA**. **This is the most important exchange in all three calls.**

2. **Chintan Mehta (Punishka Family Office, Feb-26)** — Pinned management on intercompany eliminations: "Now this problem will multifold as and when your BOO capacity gets significantly higher." Management response: "we are exploring structuring these projects in such a way that after the accounting, it can be shown as sales and it can be leased back" — confirms an accounting-engineering plan to convert BOO into "lease income" (which we now see in FY26 IP: **₹558 cr BOO/Lease income recognised**). This is potentially aggressive revenue recognition that turns capex into P&L.

3. **Aniket (individual, Nov-25)** — Asked about Rajasthan/Gujarat tenders bid at ₹1.7 lakh/MW/month — well below Pace's quoted ₹2.19-2.55 lakh. Management said "those numbers are closer to impossible… will correct in months". **6 months later in Feb-26 call Venugopal Rao admitted prices are still under pressure** ("aggressive bidding from competitors") — first guidance miss on tariff stabilisation.

**Running total: -3 / 9** (4 misses, 2 beats, 3 partial/neutral)

### Management Credibility Rating: 🟠 **MEDIUM-LOW (provisional)**

**Rationale:** Headline annual revenue/PAT guidance was met cleanly — that's an important data point given this is the first-ever year as a listed company. **BUT** every operational sub-target (40 MSEDCL sites, 5 GWh by March, container fab by April, working capital down, BESS tariff stabilisation) slipped by 1-2 quarters. This is a classic "easier to land at the consolidated number, harder to deliver on the granular operational milestones" pattern. With only 2 quarterly calls + 1 IP in the listed history, we cannot fully calibrate yet — but the early signal is **management may be over-promising on execution timelines while landing on financial guidance via accounting flexibility (BOO lease income, intercompany sales, Q4 lumpy bookings)**.

## Section 11 — What was NOT answered / dodged

1. **The full consolidated cash flow statement** — never volunteered in calls; only IP balance sheet snippets shared. Hard to verify whether the ₹558 cr "BOO lease income" is cash or accounting.
2. **Specifics of related-party transactions with Lanarsy Infra** — Nov-25 and Feb-26 calls never addressed the ₹136 cr FY25 EPC sub-contracting to promoter-controlled entity (not surprisingly — no analyst asked).
3. **Promoter Maddisetty Venugopal Rao's criminal proceedings** disclosed in RHP (4 cases, ₹7.65 mn) — not asked, not addressed.
4. **Detailed standalone vs consolidated reconciliation** — Chintan Mehta tried in Feb-26 but management answered with "we will explore leasing-back structure" — sidestepped the underlying question of whether standalone P&L deterioration is masked by intercompany sales.
5. **Specific MSEDCL VGF receipt timing** — Dinesh and Raj Mantri tried; CFO confirmed VGF schedule (10% advance, 45% on COD, 15% × 3 years) but did not confirm whether the 10% (₹40.5 cr) has actually been received.

## Section 13 — Key Takeaway (2 sentences)

Pace Digitek's management cleanly met its first-ever year of listed guidance on revenue and PAT (₹2,641 cr / 11.4% PAT margin), and built an enviable ₹11,338 cr order book in its BESS pivot — but every granular operational milestone has slipped by 1-2 quarters, working-capital and cash-conversion problems remain unresolved, and analyst grilling on BOO unit economics revealed the headline "85% EBITDA" claim collapses to ~10% PAT after depreciation and interest. **Management is delivering at the financial top-line but the operational walk has lagged the talk; second year of listed disclosures (FY27 quarterlies) is the real credibility test.**
