# Pace Digitek Ltd — Growth Triggers 1-Pager

*As of 2026-05-27 | CMP: not pulled (use Screener.in) | Mcap: post-IPO base ~₹4,000-5,000 cr indicative (issue size ₹819 cr at ~₹250-280/share) | Sources: RHP Sep 2025, 3 concalls, May 2026 IP*

## Business in one line
Telecom-infrastructure EPC house that built ₹2,400 cr revenue base on BSNL tower deployment and is now pivoting fast into utility-scale Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) manufacturing, EPC, and Build-Own-Operate (BOO) ownership for state DISCOMs and central PSUs.

---

## The Growth Triggers (5 specific catalysts)

### Trigger 1: MSEDCL 750 MW / 1500 MWh BESS BOO Project [HIGH]
- **What:** 12-year Battery Energy Storage Power Purchase Agreement (BESPA) signed Feb 21, 2025 with Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Co. Pace Renewable Energies (now under TGX) will build, own and operate BESS at 75 substations.
- **Evidence:** RHP p.143-146 ("BESPA effective from Feb 21, 2025, valid for 12 yrs"); MSEDCL Letter of Award Oct 14, 2024. First site commissioned at Balamtakali, Maharashtra on 26 Sept 2025 (Nov-25 concall, Rajavendhan P). IREDA ₹1,221.5 cr loan sanctioned June 30, 2025. ₹417 cr of IPO funds deployed by FY26-end (Q4 IP).
- **Quantum:** ₹1,258 cr executable BOO value; annuity revenue ₹197 cr/year for 12 years at ₹219,001/MW/month tariff; VGF ₹405 cr from MSEDCL/Govt (10% on FC + 45% on COD + 15% × 3 years).
- **Timeline:** Full 75-site commissioning target Aug-Sep 2026 (slipped from initial guidance of "40 sites by March 2026"). Annuity income recognition begins FY27.

### Trigger 2: BESS Manufacturing Capacity Scale-up 2.5 → 10 GWh [MEDIUM]
- **What:** Existing 2.5 GWh BESS manufacturing facility at Bidadi being expanded by +2.5 GWh (Q2 FY27) and a new 5 GWh facility coming up (Q3 FY27) — total 10 GWh by H2 FY27. Backward integration via in-house container fabrication (Q2 FY27).
- **Evidence:** Q4 FY26 IP slide 16: "Machinery received; installation and commissioning activities underway". CFO Feb-26: "₹80 crore to ₹100 crore" capex for doubling, ₹30-40 cr for container fab. Total committed.
- **Quantum:** Management indicated peak revenue potential of **₹6,000-7,000 cr** at 10 GWh utilisation (Rajiv Maddisetty, Nov-25 concall, p.11). At 80% utilisation seen in FY26, FY27 production target is 7.5 GWh, FY28 production target ~10 GWh.
- **Timeline:** Effective 5 GWh by Q2 FY27, 10 GWh by Q3 FY27. Revenue ramp FY27 onwards.

### Trigger 3: Mega Energy Order Book Conversion (₹8,854 cr) [HIGH]
- **What:** Energy order book at May 25, 2026 = ₹8,854 mn (Crores)¹. Comprises EPC ₹4,409 cr (SECI ₹1,159 cr, MAHAGENCO ₹920 cr, NLC ₹710 cr, DVC ₹702 cr, NTPC ₹584 cr, Bondada ₹334 cr) and BOO ₹4,433 cr (MSEDCL, SECI Solar+BESS, KPTCL, KREDL).
- **Evidence:** Q4 FY26 IP slide 9: itemised client-wise list including KREDL Solar+BESS BOO ₹1,775 cr — the largest order received post-Q3 FY26.
- **Quantum:** Of the ₹8,854 cr, **₹4,421 cr EPC will convert into 18-24 month revenue** (FY27-H1 FY28). BOO ₹4,433 cr will sit on the balance sheet but yield ~₹500-600 cr annuity income p.a. once fully commissioned.
- **Timeline:** EPC conversion: FY27-FY28. BOO commissioning: Aug 2026 - 2028 staggered. FY27 guidance ₹3,200-3,400 cr → FY28 ₹4,000-4,200 cr (Q4 IP).

### Trigger 4: TransGreenX Energy (TGX) HoldCo Hive-off Optionality [OPTIONALITY]
- **What:** Pace incorporated TransGreenX Energy Private Limited as a wholly-owned HoldCo for all BOO SPVs. Management has explicitly stated intent to "raise the project funding at the SPV or HoldCo level" and "at later stage, if there is a good valuation, we may consider hiving it off."
- **Evidence:** Q3 FY26 concall, Venugopal Rao Maddisetty: "At later stage, if there is a good valuation, we may consider to hiving it off. In those angles, we are working on." Confirmed structure in Q4 IP — BOO Lease income ₹558 mn recognised FY26.
- **Quantum:** Speculative. If 2.72 GWh BOO portfolio (4 projects totalling ₹4,433 cr capex) is valued at 1.5-2× book at hive-off, this could unlock ₹2,000-4,000 cr of value vs ~₹1,500 cr equity at risk — but timing & valuation are highly uncertain. **Cannot be the main reason to own.**
- **Timeline:** "Future date after the commissioning" — likely FY28+ at earliest.

### Trigger 5: Export Pivot to Africa & Middle East [OPTIONALITY]
- **What:** Pace already runs operations in Myanmar and African telecom infra (Lineage Power Holdings Singapore + Lineage Power Myanmar). In Q3 FY26 management indicated active push to export BESS to Africa and Middle East where prices are "better than India prices".
- **Evidence:** Q3 FY26 concall, Venugopal Rao: "from next financial year, we are now trying to focus on at least some portion of our production goes to the export to Africa and Middle East." No specific contract value disclosed yet.
- **Quantum:** Not quantified by management. Africa telecom power market is documented in RHP/CRISIL Report but no specific contract pipeline disclosed.
- **Timeline:** FY27 onwards — purely optionality.

## What can break the thesis (Top 3 risks)

1. **Cash-flow / working-capital trap deepens.** Receivables grew 13.5% YoY in FY26 against revenue growth of 8.3% — going the wrong way. 3-year cumulative CFO (FY23-25) ≈ -₹6 cr against PAT ₹525 cr. If BOO assets sit pre-COD or VGF/MSEDCL payments lag, the equity IRR collapses from claimed 14% to below cost-of-equity. The whole thesis depends on **clean operating cash generation** which is unproven.

2. **BESS tariff compression.** Recent state tenders bid at ₹1.7 lakh/MW/month — well below Pace's contracted ₹2.19-2.55 lakh range. Project IRR of 10-11.5% is already thin at the higher tariffs; a 20% tariff cut in new tenders would compress equity IRR to <10%. Pace's "backward integration moat" claim has only delivered 1% gross-margin uplift per management's own admission.

3. **Customer / sector concentration risk (94% telecom, 96% PSU in FY25; in FY26 ~96% Indian PSU across BSNL+BESS PSUs).** This isn't really diversification — Pace has swapped one PSU customer (BSNL) for many (SECI, NTPC, NLC, MSEDCL, KPTCL, KREDL, DVC) but the systemic exposure to (a) state-government finances, (b) L1 tendering & price discovery and (c) policy reversal remains the central risk. **Plus**, the standalone parent revenue fell 24.7% in FY26 — telecom EPC engine is shrinking even as energy ramps up; the runway between these two engines is the execution risk.

---

## Verdict

The thesis rests on two genuinely HIGH-conviction pillars (MSEDCL annuity already commissioned + Energy order book of ₹8,854 cr already signed with named PSU counterparties) backed by one MEDIUM (mfg capacity scaling on track) and two OPTIONALITY upsides (HoldCo hive-off + exports). **Net of risks, this is a credible re-rating story IF cash conversion turns positive in FY27 and BESS execution stays on calendar — but the working-capital evidence and operational milestone slippage so far suggest the multiple won't expand until investors see one full year of clean BOO annuity collections. Best treated as a WATCH with a re-look at Q2 FY27 results.**
