Executive Summary

ASM Technologies has executed a genuine structural pivot from sub-scale ER&D into capital-intensive Design-Led Manufacturing (DLM) over 8 years. FY26 was the breakout year: revenue +83% YoY, EBITDA +114%, PAT +138%, CFO turned positive at ₹68 Cr, and ROCE crossed 22%. The story is now plugged into multiple India tailwinds — semicap localisation, ECMS scheme approval, ESDM PLI, friend-shoring — and management has announced a ~₹1,500 Cr capex roadmap across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Vietnam. But valuation at ~85× FY26 P/E already prices in 3-4 more years of 40%+ growth, customer concentration is high (top 2 = 39%, top 10 = 60%), and the equity component of the announced capex remains undefined — implying material future dilution risk.

Bear Case — 5 Hard Bullets

Customer concentration is dangerously high. Two customers = 39% of FY25 revenue; top 10 = 60% of FY26. Management refuses to disclose names or splits. A single semicap OEM cancelling a programme could erase 15-20% of revenue overnight; ASM has no pricing leverage.
Valuation has run far ahead of execution. Market cap ~₹5,140 Cr on FY26 PAT ₹60.8 Cr → ~85× TTM P/E. For context: TCS ~28× · KPIT ~52× · Cyient ~25× · Avalon Tech ~50×. ASM has done one year of clean execution. One disappointing quarter could de-rate the stock 40-50%.
~₹1,500 Cr capex on a ~₹100 Cr EBITDA base. Funding is "accrual + debt + incentives + some equity". Management has been deliberately vague on equity quantum. That implies 20-40% further dilution risk over 24-36 months — possibly at lower prices. Commissioning 3 large new sites simultaneously is real execution risk.
Related-party noise. ₹10.9 Cr salary advance to MD (recurring), ₹33 Cr inter-corp loans to RV Forms LLP, large promoter warrant issuance at fixed price prior to re-rating. Standalone CFO has been negative for 2 consecutive years (FY24: -6 Cr; FY25: -235 Cr) — only consolidation cleans it up.
DLM margins may not be repeatable. Q4FY26 EBITDA margin was only 18.7% (vs 22.2% in Q4FY25); H2 was supported by operating leverage on a small revenue base. As 7 facilities ramp, gestation losses and depreciation rise. 19.1% FY26 EBITDA margin may compress 200-400 bps in FY27.

Key Takeaway (2 sentences)

ASM's first-ever investor call confirmed that the DLM pivot is real and accelerating (171% Q2 growth, DLM now 63% of mix, 80-85% utilisation, 18-24 month visibility) but management is uncomfortable with public-market disclosure expectations — no quantitative guidance, no customer split, no segment-wise revenue, no clarity on equity quantum for a ~₹760 Cr capex roadmap. The next two concalls should be judged on: (a) ER&D growth recovering as promised, (b) capacity utilisation staying >80% as new facilities come on, and (c) the equity-vs-debt mix being finally disclosed.

Verdict

WATCH — Revisit on Trigger
3-Sentence Rationale:
  1. ASM has executed a genuine, structural pivot from sub-scale ER&D to DLM — 83% revenue growth, ROCE crossing 22%, CFO turning positive, debt fully covered — plugged into right tailwinds (semicap localisation, ECMS, ESDM PLI, friend-shoring).
  2. But valuation at ~85× FY26 earnings already prices in 3-4 more years of 40%+ growth, customer concentration is high, and ~₹1,500 Cr capex will require debt and probably equity dilution that has not been quantified.
  3. Wait for one of: (a) 30-40% valuation reset, (b) clear disclosure of customer mix and equity quantum of capex, or (c) two more clean quarters that prove FY26 was not a one-customer-driven blowout.
