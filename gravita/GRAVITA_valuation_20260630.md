## Snapshot
India's largest organised lead recycler diversifying into copper, lithium, rubber and aluminium. Risk/reward on FY27 estimates is **Bear −35% / Base +16% / Bull +61%** — a favourably skewed *range*, but the Base case offers only modest upside and an EV/EBITDA cross-check flags the stock as close to fully valued. **Entry price, not thesis, is the deciding variable.**

## 5. Part Evaluation (SOTP)
**Limited applicability** — Gravita is an integrated, single-theme recycling business; segments (lead, aluminium, plastic, copper) share procurement, logistics and customers and are best valued as one entity (Section A). A light parts view: core recycling (lead/Al/plastic, ~₹420 Cr EBITDA) carries the premium multiple, while RMIL copper (~₹82 Cr EBITDA, ~8% margin) would fetch a lower metals multiple until backward integration lifts its returns — i.e. blended fair multiple should sit *below* the pure-core multiple, reinforcing the Base 30x (not 35x+) exit assumption.

## 6. Scenario Scoreboard & Verdict

| Scenario | Method | Target | Gap | What has to be true |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bear | Build + de-rate (22x) | ₹1,063 | **−35%** | Volume stuck at ~5%, commodity/FX squeeze, margin to 7%, multiple de-rates |
| Base | Build at ~30x | ₹1,884 | **+16%** | RMIL consolidates, +13% organic, margins hold ~8% PAT, multiple roughly held |
| Bull | Build + re-rate (35x) | ₹2,625 | **+61%** | Volume re-accelerates toward 20%+, VAP/copper synergies, premium re-rating |
| EV/EBITDA cross-check | 18x EBITDA | ₹1,441 | −12% | enterprise-basis fair value |

**Verdict.** Risk/reward is **−35% (Bear) to +61% (Bull)** with a Base of **+16%** — a positively skewed *range* but a thin Base margin of safety. The business quality is high (net-debt-free, 24%+ ROIC, AA-, real tailwind), yet at ~32x TTM P/E the price already discounts much of the near-term growth, and the EV/EBITDA cross-check (−12%) warns that the equity multiple is doing a lot of the work. The single biggest swing factor is **volume growth re-accelerating from FY26's disappointing +5% toward the guided double-digits** — that, not margins, is what separates Bull from Bear. **Most probable: Base (modest upside).** A patient buyer is better served accumulating on de-rating toward the ₹1,350-1,450 zone (where EV/EBITDA and Section-B de-rate converge) than chasing at CMP.
