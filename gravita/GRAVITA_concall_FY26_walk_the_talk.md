# Gravita India — Concall Analysis (Walk-the-Talk + Q4FY26 Brief)

**Transcripts analysed:** Q1FY26 (Jul 2025), Q2FY26 (Nov 2025), Q3FY26 (Jan 2026), Q4FY26 (08 May 2026)
**Management:** Yogesh Malhotra (WTD & CEO), Sunil Kansal (WTD & CFO), Naveen Sharma (ED)

## Part 1 — Q4FY26 Concall Brief (9 sections)

**1. Headline numbers (consolidated, FY26 / Q4FY26).**
- FY26 Revenue **₹4,265 Cr** (+10% YoY); Adjusted EBITDA **₹452.48 Cr** (+12% YoY), margin 10.6%; PAT **₹378.80 Cr** (+21% YoY), margin 8.88%.
- Q4FY26 Revenue **₹1,172.76 Cr** (+13% YoY, +15% QoQ); Adj EBITDA **₹112.91 Cr** (+4% YoY), margin 9.63%; PAT **₹91.88 Cr**, margin 7.83%.
- 5-yr CAGR (FY21-26): Revenue 25%, EBITDA 49%, PAT 31%.

**2. Segment performance.** FY26 total volume +5% to 56,208 MT. Lead sales +7% to 48,889 MT (capacity additions + stabilisation). Aluminium *declined* — "inability to hedge and a selective sales strategy"; expected to recover once MCX hedging (ADC12) goes live. EBITDA/ton FY26: Lead ₹23,043, Plastic ₹16,043, Aluminium ₹12,328.

**3. Demand commentary.** Industry undergoing formalisation (informal→formal), tightening regulation improving domestic scrap availability and compliance-led sourcing. Healthy order book.

**4. Margin drivers / pressures.** Drivers: higher value-added mix (42% of revenue), domestic scrap, operating leverage. Pressure: aluminium hedging gap; some product-mix softness in Q4 ("EBITDA per ton came down to some extent").

**5. Capex & balance sheet.** FY26 capex ₹372 Cr. Total capex earmarked **₹1,700 Cr through FY29** (₹815 Cr existing businesses, balance for lithium/copper/steel). Net-debt-free; CFO flagged "may take limited debt." Steady-state tax rate guided **17-18%**.

**6. Guidance (verbatim, tagged).**
- *"We continue to work towards our medium-term target of scaling this up to over 8 lakh metric per annum by FY '29"* — **MEDIUM**
- *"Commercial operations [copper Mandvi plant] are expected to commence within the next 12 months"* — **MEDIUM**
- *"overall, in the next three years, we are very confident"* (of growth) — **MEDIUM-HIGH**
- *"tax rate for going forward should be in the range of 17%-18%"* — **MEDIUM**
- Working-capital days *"should be close to 85 to 90 days going forward"* — **MEDIUM**

**7. New announcements.** Acquired **99.44% of Rashtriya Metal Industries Ltd (RMIL)** for ₹560 Cr (copper, 31,200 MTPA, 40% exports). New **copper recycling plant at Mandvi** (29,400 MTPA Phase 1, ~₹160 Cr, 12 months). Li-ion 6,000 MTPA pilot commissioned (Jan 2026). Mundra lead +80,300 to 145,100 MTPA (Feb 2026). ESG rating 65 (NSC).

**8. Analyst Q&A — sharpest exchanges.**
- *Q (Emkay): Is lead capacity being rationalised given copper capex?* → *"No, we are not changing the capacity of lead… earlier the plan was 700,000 tons but now we are taking it to 800,000 tons. The additional capacity is coming up in copper."*
- *Q: Commissioning of the 45,000t lead capacity slated for Q4?* → *"It is going to happen in this Q1 only in Jaipur… waiting for the government approvals"* (a slip from Q4FY26).
- *Q: RMIL margins ~8% vs Gravita ~10% — can they be raised?* → *"in RMIL… definitely we would plan to increase this"* via backward integration (own copper plant).

**9. Key takeaway.** FY26 was a year of strong profit growth (PAT +21%) and aggressive diversification (copper, lithium) even as headline *volume* growth (+5%) badly lagged the 25% aspiration. Margins again beat the company's own conservative ₹19-20/kg lead guidance (~₹23/kg actual); the story is increasingly about value-added mix and new verticals rather than raw lead tonnage.

### Management Credibility Rating: **MEDIUM-HIGH**

**Pattern observed:** Management is **conservative and reliable on margins** (under-promises ₹19-20/kg, over-delivers ₹23/kg) and **executes on capacity, acquisitions and ESG milestones**. The recurring credibility gap is the **25% volume-growth narrative**, repeated on every call while actual FY26 volume grew only 5% — softened by reframing it as a multi-year CAGR. Capex and capacity targets have also been revised upward/outward, though these are growth-driven (copper) rather than shortfalls. Net: trustworthy operators worth taking at their word on margins and project delivery, but discount the headline volume-growth claims and watch the slipping near-term capacity timelines.

## Confidence-Language Audit (FY26 calls)
- **HIGH conviction** used appropriately for completed actions ("we have acquired", "we commissioned").
- **MEDIUM** dominates forward guidance ("we expect", "we are confident", "targeting") — reasonable.
- ⚠️ **Watch:** The most material recurring claim (25% volume growth) sits at MEDIUM-HIGH conviction but has the weakest delivery — the classic "repeated optimism without near-term delivery" pattern, partially mitigated by honest acknowledgement of the single-digit prints.

## What was dodged / not fully answered
- Persistent analyst pressure to **upgrade the lead margin guidance** (peers already have) — management deflects, "wait six more months."
- Precise FY27 volume guidance — declined, deferred to "H1 next year once India plants ramp."
