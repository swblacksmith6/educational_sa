# Gravita India — Growth Triggers 1-Pager
*As of 30 Jun 2026 | CMP: ₹1,631 | Mcap: ₹12,045 Cr | Source: Screener.in*

## Business in one line
India's largest organised lead recycler, now diversifying into a multi-metal (aluminium, plastic, rubber, copper, lithium) recycling and value-added-products platform riding the formalisation of India's scrap economy — net-debt-free, AA- rated, targeting 8 lakh MTPA by FY29.

---

## The Growth Triggers

### Trigger 1: Copper entry via RMIL acquisition + Mandvi plant [HIGH]
- **What:** Acquired 99.44% of Rashtriya Metal Industries Ltd (copper/alloys, 31,200 MTPA, 40% exports) for ₹560 Cr; building a captive copper recycling plant at Mandvi (29,400 MTPA Phase-1).
- **Evidence:** Q4FY26 concall (08 May 2026): *"Gravita has entered the copper segment through the acquisition of 99.44% stake in RMIL for INR 560 crores… RMIL reported revenue of ~₹1,040 Cr and EBITDA of ~₹82 Cr for FY26."* Mandvi: *"capex of INR 160 crores… commercial operations expected within the next 12 months."*
- **Quantum:** RMIL adds ~₹1,040 Cr revenue immediately; backward integration via Mandvi targets RMIL EBITDA margin lift from ~8% toward Gravita's ~10%+.
- **Timeline:** RMIL consolidating from FY27; Mandvi by FY27.

### Trigger 2: Lead capacity ramp — 4.57 → 8+ lakh MTPA [HIGH on commissioned, MEDIUM on pipeline]
- **What:** Mundra lead capacity expanded +80,300 to 145,100 MTPA (Feb 2026); total installed now ~4.57 lakh MTPA, target 8+ lakh MTPA by FY29.
- **Evidence:** Q4FY26 concall: *"total install capacity now at about 4.57 lakh metric ton per annum… medium-term target of scaling this up to over 8 lakh metric per annum by FY '29."*
- **Quantum:** ~75% volume headroom over 3 years; at ~₹23,000/ton lead EBITDA, material EBITDA accretion as utilisation ramps.
- **Timeline:** FY27-FY29 (note: 45,000t Jaipur line slipped to Q1FY27).

### Trigger 3: Value-added product mix to 50% [MEDIUM]
- **What:** Shift mix from 42% (FY26) toward 45-50% value-added (customised alloys, red lead, lead sheets, copper foils, TPO).
- **Evidence:** Q4FY26: *"Value-added products contributed 42%… target is a 50% contribution."* Q2FY26: *"value-added products give you a 2.5% to 3% additional gross margin."*
- **Quantum:** ~250-300 bps gross-margin uplift on the converted volume.
- **Timeline:** Progressive through FY27-FY29.

### Trigger 4: Formalisation of scrap economy (EPR / BWMR / scrappage / GST) [MEDIUM]
- **What:** Regulation shifting scrap from informal to formal players — more domestic scrap availability, lower working capital, better utilisation for organised recyclers like Gravita.
- **Evidence:** Q4FY26: *"the industry is undergoing supply chain formalization and enhanced compliance lead sourcing."* IP "Paradigm Shift" — BWMR, EPR, Vehicle Scrappage Policy, GST.
- **Quantum:** Structural — improves scrap cost, WC cycle (guided 85-90 days) and ROIC durability.
- **Timeline:** Multi-year secular.

### Trigger 5: Aluminium recovery on MCX ADC12 hedging [MEDIUM]
- **What:** Aluminium volumes fell in FY26 due to inability to hedge; expected to recover once MCX ADC12 contract enables back-to-back hedging.
- **Evidence:** Q4FY26: *"aluminum segment witnessed a declining trend… volumes are expected to pick up once the hedging mechanism is live on MCX."*
- **Quantum:** Restores a depressed segment; EBITDA/ton ~₹12,000-15,000.
- **Timeline:** FY27, contingent on exchange launch.

### Trigger 6: Rubber/tyre recycling (Europe + Mundra) [MEDIUM]
- **What:** Romania tyre plant ramping; Mundra rubber project commissioning.
- **Evidence:** Q3FY26: *"Mundra rubber project slated for commissioning in Q1 FY '27, with revenues expected from Q2 FY '27."*
- **Quantum:** New revenue line; early-stage.
- **Timeline:** Revenue from H1FY27.

### Trigger 7: Lithium-ion battery recycling [OPTIONALITY]
- **What:** 6,000 MTPA pilot commissioned at Mundra (Jan 2026, ₹14 Cr); scale-up as EV battery feedstock builds.
- **Evidence:** Q4FY26: *"commissioned a 6,000 metric ton per annum pilot lithium-ion battery recycling facility… expected to scale up gradually as operations stabilize."*
- **Quantum:** Immaterial today; large long-dated EV-recycling option.
- **Timeline:** FY28+ optionality. *Not a reason to own today.*

## What can break the thesis (top 3 risks)
1. **Valuation reset** — at 31.8x TTM P/E, any disappointment on volume (FY26 grew only 5% vs 25% aspiration) or margin compression de-rates the stock sharply.
2. **Commodity/FX cycle** — lead & aluminium spreads and the rupee drive earnings; a sustained spread squeeze hits EBITDA despite hedging.
3. **Diversification execution** — simultaneous copper, lithium, rubber and steel build-out across geographies risks integration slip, goodwill, and capex overrun (capex already revised ₹1,200→₹1,700 Cr).

---

## Verdict
The two HIGH-conviction triggers — the closed RMIL copper acquisition and the already-commissioning lead-capacity ramp — together justify the growth thesis on their own, supported by a genuine, data-backed formalisation tailwind and a margin-accretive value-added mix shift. Lithium is pure optionality and should not anchor the case. The thesis is sound; the live question is price — at 31.8x, much of the near-term growth is already in the stock, so entry discipline (see valuation) matters more than thesis conviction here.
