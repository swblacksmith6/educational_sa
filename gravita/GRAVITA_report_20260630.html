DuPont / ROE

ROIC stepped down from ~31% (FY24) to ~24-27% (FY25-26) as the QIP-enlarged equity base and capex-in-progress dilute near-term returns. ROE = net margin (~7-8%) × asset turnover (moderating) × leverage (falling, net-debt-free) ⇒ high-teens ROE, down from low-20s pre-QIP. Thesis: volume + value-added mix re-accelerate returns toward 25%+ ROIC.

Bear Case

  1. Premium valuation — 31.8x P/E, 4.9x book for a recycler whose ROIC is declining (31%→24%) leaves no margin of safety.
  2. Commodity + FX cyclicality — earnings ride LME lead/aluminium and the rupee; hedging smooths but a spread squeeze hits fast.
  3. Acquisition-led diversification risk — copper, Europe tyre, lithium, steel stretch management; integration + goodwill risk.
  4. Accounting/governance blemish — standing qualified audit opinion at a governance-marketed company.
  5. Capital intensity & dilution history — 8 lakh MTPA needs sustained capex; FY25 QIP shows the model can require external equity.
