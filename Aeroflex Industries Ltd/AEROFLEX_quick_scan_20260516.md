## Step 2 – 12-Point Annual Report Analysis

**1. What does the company do?**
Aeroflex Industries (incorporated 1993) is India's leading manufacturer of stainless-steel "flexible flow solutions" — corrugated SS flexible hoses (braided & unbraided), interlock hoses, composite hoses, assemblies & fittings, and (newly) metal & miniature bellows. Its products transfer liquids, gases, and solids across high-pressure / high-temperature / vibration-prone applications. End uses span oil & gas, steel, chemicals, refineries, plus new-age verticals: aerospace, defence, hydrogen, semiconductors, AI data-centre liquid cooling, EV thermal management, fire-fighting, robotics, solar.

**2. Business segments & revenue mix (FY25)**
- SS Flexible Hoses (with & without braiding): **48%**
- Assemblies, Fittings & Bellows (high-margin value-add): **52%** (vs. ~45% earlier; targeted >55% in FY26)
- Skid assemblies — emerging vertical, expected to scale to 20–22% of revenue in FY27 (mgmt guidance)
- Metal bellows — capacity of 1.2 lakh pcs commissioned Jan-2025; for AI data-centre liquid cooling + bellows-tipped products

**3. Geography mix (FY25)**
- Exports: **75%** of revenue (90+ countries)
- Domestic: **25%** (rising to 31% by Q4FY26)
- Region-wise (international): Americas 56% (US alone 47% of exports), Europe 30%, Asia 10%, Africa 3%, Others 1%
- Domestic share of total revenue grew to ~26% in FY25 and to ~31% by Q4FY26 — de-risking export concentration

**4. Customer concentration**
- DRHP-era (FY23): Top-5 = 24.1% of revenue; Top-10 = 31.4% — **moderate, declining** (was ~33% in FY22)
- Multi-national OEMs (US, Europe), with brand-name domestic clients: ONGC, Tata Steel, Tata Metaliks, Kirloskar Pneumatics, Blue Star, DCM Shriram, IndianOil, Pidilite, Welspun
- Supplier concentration (FY23): Top-5 = 74.5% of raw material — **HIGH** (key risk; 43% of raw material from China)

**5. Promoter / management background & pledging**
- Promoter holding: **~67.0%** (Mar-2026) via Aeroflex Enterprises Ltd (formerly Sat Industries Ltd, separately listed: AEROENTER)
- Pledge: **0% pledged** (confirmed FY26)
- MD & Chairman: **Asad Daud, 35**, MSc London School of Economics, executive education at IIM-B / ISB / Harvard. Young, ambitious; promoter-family.
- WTD & CFO: **Mustafa Abid Kachwala**, 57, with the company since 2010
- Non-Exec Director: **Harikant Turgalia**, 63, associated with promoter group since 2001
- 4 independent directors out of 7; 97.96% board meeting attendance
- Daud family is also promoter of multiple other listed entities (Aeroflex Enterprises, Sah Polymers, etc.) — group corporate structure to be watched

**6. Capex cycle status — EXPANSION**
- FY25 capex: **₹103 Cr** (CWIP ₹9.8 Cr, gross block jumped from ₹82 Cr to ₹170 Cr)
- New facility: Metal Bellows unit at Lodha Industrial Park Palava II (68,459 sq.ft.) — production live Jan-2025
- New facility: MIDC Chakan (Pune) for skid assemblies and Hyd-Air integration
- Hyd-Air Engineering acquired in FY25 — adds hydraulic fittings, valves, fluid connectors (German technology); serves railways, shipbuilding, heavy industries
- Total capacity: 16.5 Mn metres hoses + 6 composite hose lines + 40 assembly stations + 1.2 lakh metal bellow pcs

- New verticals: AI data-centre liquid cooling (via US partnership), aerospace, hydrogen, EV, semiconductor, fire-fighting, robotics
- Capacity: bellows facility live; Hyd-Air integration; skid assemblies as next major growth lever (20–22% of FY27 revenue per concall)
- Margin expansion: EBITDA margin target ~23% (achieved Q4FY26 = 23.86%); LT target 24%+
- Domestic share to grow further

## Step 5 – Bear Case (5 sharpest arguments **against** owning)

1. **Valuation is extreme.** Current P/E ~87x trailing FY26 PAT of ₹55.5 Cr on a market cap of ~₹4,931 Cr. P/B ~14x book value of ~₹342 Cr. For the stock to "just hold" at current levels, the company needs **35–40% earnings CAGR for 5+ years** — leaves zero margin for execution slip-up.
2. **Export concentration to US + tariff risk.** 47% of total exports go to the US, and ~75% of revenue is exports. Trump-era tariff uncertainty plus 43% raw-material imports from China create a two-sided geopolitical squeeze. A 10% USD-INR adverse move + 10% tariff = potentially 200–300 bps EBITDA hit.
3. **Working capital is creeping.** Receivables grew faster than revenue 2 years running; cash conversion (CFO/PAT) dropped to 51%. As exports scale further, debtor days could continue stretching — funded by IPO cash today, but not sustainable as the business doubles.
4. **Small base, "premium-multiple-stays-only-if-skids-work."** FY26 revenue is ₹443 Cr. Mgmt guides 35% growth in FY27 with skid assemblies scaling to 20–22% of revenue. This is a guidance-execution stock. If skid assemblies under-deliver or if Hyd-Air integration disappoints, multiple compresses sharply.
5. **Promoter group complexity & RPTs.** Promoter (Aeroflex Enterprises, formerly Sat Industries) is itself listed; related-party sales, machine maintenance fees, and CSR routing to family foundation create governance optical issues — not material today, but worth tracking with a young 35-year-old MD who controls the cap table.

## Step 6 – Verdict

### **WATCH (revisit on trigger)**

**Justification:** Aeroflex is a high-quality, near-debt-free, export-led small-cap with structural tailwinds in flexible flow solutions, a credible margin-expansion thesis (assemblies/bellows/skids), and zero promoter pledge. However, the current valuation (87x P/E, 14x P/B) prices in flawless execution for 5+ years, leaving no margin of safety on a stock where the bear case (US tariff risk, WC bloat, small base) is non-trivial. Strategically attractive, tactically expensive.

**Move to BUY-WORTHY if any of:**
- Q1/Q2 FY27 prints validate skid assemblies scaling toward 20%+ contribution
- Stock corrects to <60x trailing PE or <8x EV/Sales on FY27 estimates
- Domestic mix crosses 35% (de-risks export/USD/tariff exposure)
- Hyd-Air full year contribution adds clear EBITDA margin lift (>200 bps)
