# AEROFLEX INDUSTRIES — Growth Triggers 1-Pager
*As of 16-May-2026 | CMP ≈ ₹382 | Mcap ≈ ₹4,931 Cr | Source: Screener.in / Trendlyne (post-Q4FY26)*

## Business in one line
India's largest exporter of stainless-steel flexible flow solutions (hoses, assemblies, fittings, metal bellows) — pivoting from commodity hoses to high-margin assemblies and AI-data-centre liquid cooling skid systems.

---

## The 6 Growth Triggers

### Trigger 1: Liquid Cooling Skid Assemblies for Data Centers [HIGH]
- **What:** Exclusive 5-year contract with India subsidiary of a listed US corp (~USD 70 Bn mcap; likely Vertiv/Schneider) to manufacture skid assemblies + advanced flow-control components for AI data-center liquid cooling. First dispatch in Q3FY26.
- **Evidence:** FY26 skid sales = ₹21.2 Cr from 617 units in 4 months (Q4FY26 concall, 6-May-2026, p.3). Capacity scaling 2,000 → 6,000 (May-26 live) → 15,000 units by Q2 FY27. "At 75% capacity utilization, we can expect a total revenue for the business to come in at about ₹325 crores to about ₹330-odd crores." (May-26 concall, p.10)
- **Quantum:** ~₹300–325 Cr at peak (FY29). FY27 contribution ~20–22% of revenue (mgmt guide).
- **Timeline:** FY26 = ₹21 Cr → FY27 ~₹100 Cr → FY28-29 = peak.
- **Status:** **On track, ramp slower than originally signalled due to customer-audit / design bottlenecks (admitted May-26).**

### Trigger 2: Margin Expansion via Value-Added Mix (Hoses → Assemblies → Bellows) [HIGH]
- **What:** Strategic shift from low-margin braided hoses (commodity) to high-margin assemblies, fittings, metal bellows and skids. Already 52–54% of revenue.
- **Evidence:** FY26 value-added mix 52% (vs 45% in FY24); Q4FY26 EBITDA margin **23.86%** vs FY25 21.5% — **+326 bps Y-o-Y in Q4** (Q4FY26 concall, p.3). Mgmt LT target: "25% over the next 4 to 5 years."
- **Quantum:** Every 100 bps margin expansion on FY27e revenue of ~₹600 Cr = ₹6 Cr incremental EBITDA. 200–300 bps lift through FY28 = ₹12–18 Cr/yr.
- **Timeline:** FY27 EBITDA margin guide ~23% (May-26). LT: 24–25% by FY28-29.
- **Status:** **On track; Q4FY26 already trending toward target.**

### Trigger 3: Hose Capacity Expansion (16.5 → 20 Mn metres p.a.) [HIGH]
- **What:** Hose capacity at 17.5 Mn m as of Jan-26 (1 Mn metres added); further 2.5 Mn m to come by Q2 FY27 → total 20 Mn m. Capex ₹54 Cr (announced Jan-25).
- **Evidence:** "We added 1 million meters of hose capacity in this month [Jan-26], which takes our total installed capacity to 17.5 million meters per annum. The balance 2.5 million meters will be commissioned in a phased manner and is expected to be completed by Q2 of the next financial year." (Q3FY26 concall, 29-Jan-2026, p.3). Capacity utilization Q3FY26: **71.2%** on hoses.
- **Quantum:** Peak revenue at 20 Mn m (with 70% assembly mix) = **₹650–675 Cr** (Q2FY26 concall guidance, p.4).
- **Timeline:** Q2 FY27 commissioned → FY28 full utilization.
- **Status:** **On track.**

### Trigger 4: Hyd-Air Engineering Integration & Capex [MEDIUM]
- **What:** FY25 acquisition of Hyd-Air Engineering (German-technology hydraulic fittings, fluid connectors, valves) — serves railways, shipbuilding, heavy industries; will be redeployed for internal use in skid + HVAC + DC connectors. Capex expansion planned (not yet disclosed).
- **Evidence:** FY26 Hyd-Air revenue ₹31.64 Cr (60% utilization) — matched the ₹32–35 Cr peak guidance. Mgmt: "Hyd-Air capex is planned soon. We'll be able to share the numbers in due course of time." (Q3FY26 concall, p.5). Peak post-capex guided ~₹45–50 Cr.
- **Quantum:** Standalone ₹45–50 Cr at peak + significant pull-through to skid assemblies + DC connectors.
- **Timeline:** FY27 ramp; capex announcement pending.
- **Status:** **On track; awaiting fresh capex announcement.**

### Trigger 5: Domestic Mix Increase + India-EU FTA Tailwind [MEDIUM]
- **What:** Domestic share rising from 25% (FY25) → 31% (Q4FY26) → guided higher in FY27. Driven by skid (100% domestic), Hyd-Air domestic OEMs (steel, railways, ports). India-UK FTA + India-EU FTA = tariff parity vs Turkish competitors.
- **Evidence:** "Domestic sales contribution has also increased to 31% from 26%, mainly driven by the increased traction coming in from the skid assemblies and also from Hyd-Air" (Q4FY26 concall, p.4). Mgmt on EU: "this FTA being signed and obviously, it will come into operations in the next few quarters, we definitely feel that it's going to be a big boost for us…manufacturers based out of Turkey who are getting duty-free material to be exported to EU." (Q3FY26 concall, p.6).
- **Quantum:** De-risks US tariff exposure (US is 25–26% of revenue); incremental domestic + EU growth ~₹40–60 Cr in FY27.
- **Timeline:** FY27 onwards.
- **Status:** **Trending well; FTA implementation date is the trigger.**

### Trigger 6: Metal Bellows Plant + ASME Certification [MEDIUM]
- **What:** New 1.2 Lakh-piece bellows plant live at Lodha Industrial Park since Jan-2025. ASME (American Society of Mechanical Engineers) Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code certification received — opens up US data-center, hydrogen, aerospace, pressure-equipment markets.
- **Evidence:** "Received certification from the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) under the Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code." (Q3FY26 IP, Jan-26, p.12). Bellows annual run-rate ₹12 Cr (Q3FY26) targeting ₹36 Cr run-rate within "few quarters". Peak revenue ~₹85 Cr by FY28-29 (mgmt admitted behind original schedule).
- **Quantum:** ~₹85 Cr at peak utilization (FY29). Margins at peak: 28–30% EBITDA (gross 50–60%).
- **Timeline:** Phased ramp through FY27-29.
- **Status:** **On track but slower than original — biggest disappointment so far.**

## What can break the thesis (top 3 risks)

1. **Valuation already discounting flawless execution.** Trailing P/E ~87x and P/B ~14x leave zero margin of safety. Any miss on FY27 guidance (35% growth, 23% margin) or slower skid ramp could compress the multiple 30-40% even if business stays intact.
2. **US tariff escalation + customer-onboarding delays.** US = 25–26% of revenue; tariffs at 50% in Q3 FY26 + slow new-customer onboarding are real near-term drags. If US-India trade tensions persist, FY27 export growth could disappoint despite the FTA tailwind.
3. **Skid assemblies are single-customer / single-product line concentration risk.** The ₹300+ Cr peak skid revenue is dependent on one exclusive principal in India (no royalty, but no patent either). Counterparty risk + design IP risk (design is being done by Aeroflex teams now, raising "what's the moat?" question) are real over a 3-5 year view.

## Verdict
The 3 HIGH-conviction triggers (skids ramp, margin mix, hose capex) alone justify a credible 2.5x revenue thesis from FY26 base by FY28-29, with EBITDA potentially scaling from ₹100 Cr to ₹250-280 Cr. Add the MEDIUM-conviction tailwinds (Hyd-Air, domestic mix, bellows, India-EU FTA) and there is sufficient breadth that one or two triggers slipping doesn't break the thesis. The OPTIONALITY layer (international skid exports, inorganic) is genuine upside.

The thesis is **fundamentally sound** but **valuation is the gating factor**. Stock works as a "WATCH at 87x P/E; BUY-WORTHY on 15-25% correction OR FY27 Q1-Q2 prints validating skid scale-up." Current price already prices in 5 years of perfect execution; the asymmetry on upside vs downside is unfavourable at CMP.
