# Netweb Technologies — Growth Triggers 1-Pager
*As of 16-May-2026 | CMP: ₹3,888 | M-cap: ₹22,250 cr | Source: Screener.in / BSE/NSE filings*

## Business in one line
India's only full-stack, in-house OEM for high-end computing systems (HPC + Private Cloud + AI servers), built under "Make in India" — manufacturing NVIDIA Blackwell GPU systems and supercomputers for IIT, ISRO, defence, IndiaAI Mission, and large enterprise customers like TCS, Infosys, Yotta, Zoho.

> _Explainer — How to read this page._ Each trigger lists **what** the catalyst is, **evidence** from filings/concall, **quantum** in rupees/% with the likely revenue / EBITDA / PAT impact, and a **conviction tag** (HIGH / MEDIUM / OPTIONALITY). HIGH = money basically on the way. MEDIUM = announced with data. OPTIONALITY = plausible but uncommitted. Sum of HIGH+MEDIUM should justify the thesis on its own; OPTIONALITY is upside.

## The 6 Growth Triggers

### Trigger 1: Execution of the ₹1,734 cr IndiaAI sovereign-AI order   [HIGH]
- **What:** Single contract under IndiaAI Mission to build Blackwell-based GPU servers for the country's sovereign AI compute. Manufactured by Netweb in India, deployed via Tyrone Camarero AI platform. Of the original ₹2,184 cr two-order package, ~₹450 cr was shipped in Q3 FY26; **~₹1,600 cr remains for Q1–Q3 FY27**.
- **Evidence:** Q3 FY26 concall (19-Jan-26): _"During the quarter, Netweb successfully executed a large strategic order valued at ₹4,504 million."_ Q4 FY26 concall (4-May-26): _"Within the next 3 quarters, we expect strategic orders to be done… ₹1,600 crores is the strategic order remaining."_
- **Quantum:** Revenue +₹1,600 cr over FY27 (≈73% of FY26 revenue). Strategic orders carry **slightly thinner margins than core (~13% EBITDA vs base 14%+)** but are LC-backed, so collection risk is near-zero.
  - **Revenue impact:** +73% revenue contribution from this order alone in FY27.
  - **EBITDA impact:** ~₹208 cr at 13% margin.
  - **PAT impact:** ~₹150 cr at ~9.3% margin (~73% accretive to FY26 PAT).
- **Timeline:** Q1 FY27 through Q3 FY27.
- **Status:** Execution underway; inventory pre-stocked (₹804 cr inventory at Mar-26).

### Trigger 2: IndiaAI Mission roadmap expansion — 10,000 → 100,000 GPUs   [MEDIUM]
- **What:** Government of India's IndiaAI Mission initially budgeted for **10,000 GPUs**; this was raised to **25,000 GPUs**, and management now indicates GoI is **prepared to go to 100,000 GPUs**. Netweb is one of two large Indian OEMs eligible to bid (vs international OEMs).
- **Evidence:** Q4 FY26 concall (4-May-26): _"They were talking about 10,000 GPUs which became 25,000 GPUs. Now they are even ready to go up to 100,000 GPUs… government's focus is very clear."_ Total IndiaAI Mission budget: **₹10,300 cr** (per Q1 FY26 IP).
- **Quantum:** Even at 30–35% share of incremental ~75,000 GPUs over FY27–29, that is a **₹3,000–4,000 cr opportunity** beyond the current strategic backlog. Realistically split across multiple OEMs.
  - **Revenue impact:** +₹1,000–1,500 cr p.a. across FY28–30 if Netweb wins ~30% share.
  - **EBITDA impact:** ~₹130–195 cr p.a. additional.
  - **PAT impact:** ~₹93–140 cr p.a. additional.
- **Timeline:** Tendering FY27–28; revenue FY27–FY30.
- **Status:** Pipeline acknowledged but no specific RFP win disclosed yet.

### Trigger 3: ₹4,400 cr organic pipeline conversion   [MEDIUM]
- **What:** Apart from the strategic AI order, management has disclosed a **₹4,400 cr active organic pipeline** of which **~60% historically converts** to firm orders over 18–24 months.
- **Evidence:** Q4 FY26 concall (4-May-26): _"Order book ₹2,100 cr + L1 ₹300 cr = ₹2,400 cr… plus ₹4,400 cr of pipeline. So that stacks up to ₹6,500 cr total… normally 60% of pipeline gets converted over 18 months."_
- **Quantum:** Implied **₹2,640 cr** in converted organic order flow over 18 months (60% of ₹4,400 cr) — incremental to the ₹2,400 cr starting backlog.
  - **Revenue impact:** Underpins management's 35–40% **organic** growth guidance for FY27–28. Organic revenue would scale from ~₹620 cr (FY26 ex-strategic) to ~₹1,500 cr+ by FY28.
  - **EBITDA impact:** Higher-margin (organic mix is HPC + Private Cloud at ~14–15%) — could add ₹220+ cr EBITDA at the run-rate.
  - **PAT impact:** Roughly ~₹160 cr incremental at 10–10.5% margin.
- **Timeline:** FY27 and FY28.
- **Status:** Pipeline tracked quarterly. No single contract identified yet, but the company has converted ~60% of stated pipeline historically.

### Trigger 4: Tyrone Camarero AI platform + NVIDIA Blackwell GB200 manufacturing partnership   [HIGH]
- **What:** Netweb is the **only Indian OEM** manufacturing NVIDIA GB200 NVL4 platforms under "Make in India". Tyrone Camarero is its productised platform brand for generative AI / foundation-model training. Direct NVIDIA OEM relationship with Blackwell allocation.
- **Evidence:** AR25 page 13: _"Established a roadmap to design and develop AI GPU systems based on cutting-edge NVIDIA Blackwell platform under OEM Partnership with NVIDIA in Q3 FY2025."_ Multiple Q3-Q4 FY26 commentary references Blackwell allocation in hand.
- **Quantum:** Pricing power on every AI rack vs commodity ODMs. **Allows Netweb to capture sovereign + private AI infra opportunity at a TAM of ₹50,000+ cr over 5 years** (multiple industry estimates) where being an indigenous OEM is a precondition for government RFPs.
  - **Revenue impact:** Sustains 30%+ growth rate beyond FY28 (i.e., extends the runway).
  - **EBITDA impact:** Defends 13–14% margin even as commoditisation risk grows.
  - **PAT impact:** Protects 9–10% PAT margin floor; otherwise margins could compress to mid-single-digits.
- **Timeline:** Already operational; relevance through FY30.
- **Status:** Active. New 15,000 sq ft liquid-cooled SMT facility commissioned FY26 specifically for Blackwell-class density.

### Trigger 5: Margin recovery from working-capital normalisation   [MEDIUM]
- **What:** ROCE / ROE have inflected sharply upward (32.9% / 37.5% in FY26 vs 24% / 32.6% in FY25). Asset turnover doubled from 1.6x → 2.7x. Once the IndiaAI strategic order ships and ₹270 cr of WC borrowings unwind, operating leverage compounds.
- **Evidence:** Q4 FY26 concall — Ankit Singhal: _"The high top-line growth and we are still sustaining EBITDA margin — that primarily gives the validation and the resilience in margins. The leverage is already embedded."_ AR25 page 43: ROCE 32.6%; per FY26 FY-end IP: ROCE 37.5%.
- **Quantum:** As CCC normalises from 84 → 60–70 days, ₹150–200 cr of working capital frees up, eliminating need for short-term borrowing and lifting **interest coverage** while ROIC steps up.
  - **Revenue impact:** No direct impact — this is a quality-of-earnings trigger.
  - **EBITDA impact:** Modest (saving ~₹15–20 cr p.a. on interest).
  - **PAT impact:** **+₹15–20 cr p.a. via lower finance cost; the bigger impact is on free-cash-flow and ROCE**, which can re-rate the multiple.
- **Timeline:** H2 FY27.
- **Status:** Inventory days rising in Q4 due to strategic-order pre-stock; expected to normalise as orders ship.

### Trigger 6: AMD Turin / Intel Emerald Rapids "Make in India" platforms + HPC tailwind   [OPTIONALITY]
- **What:** Beyond NVIDIA, Netweb has built CPU server platforms on AMD Turin (5th-gen EPYC) and Intel Emerald Rapids — addressing HPC and Agentic AI / inference workloads which are CPU-heavy. National Supercomputing Mission 2.0 (NSM 2.0) is the parallel government tailwind for HPC.
- **Evidence:** Q4 FY26 concall — Sanjay Lodha: _"HPC has grown by around 31%… because of NSM 2.0 plus adoption of HPC and AI together across automobile, oil & gas industries."_
- **Quantum:** HPC segment grew 31% in FY26 to ~₹520 cr; potential to grow at 25%+ annually for the next 3 years as NSM 2.0 deploys and Agentic AI lifts CPU demand.
  - **Revenue impact:** HPC segment scaling from ₹520 cr → ₹1,000+ cr by FY29.
  - **EBITDA impact:** HPC carries higher gross margins than AI — protects blended margin.
  - **PAT impact:** Supports normalised PAT margin at 10%+.
- **Timeline:** FY27–FY30.
- **Status:** Underway but smaller in dollar terms than Triggers 1–4. Hyperscaler chip diversification (Google TPU, AWS Trainium) is also an optionality — Netweb is "open to designing for them if there is significant adoption" but not signed.

## What can break the thesis (top 3 risks)

1. **Single-order slippage in IndiaAI execution.** ~73% of incremental FY27 revenue is tied to one ₹1,600 cr remaining strategic shipment. If GoI delays acceptance / payment by 2 quarters, FY27 PAT could miss by 25–30%. Mitigant: LC-backed, no credit risk; only timing.

2. **NVIDIA Blackwell allocation cut due to US export controls.** Netweb has no captive silicon. Any US restriction on advanced GPU shipments to non-aligned countries could throttle the AI segment. Mitigant: AMD/Intel alternates already in production, but those serve different workloads.

3. **Valuation re-rating risk on margin disappointment.** At 108x TTM PE and 82x EV/EBITDA, even a single quarter of margin miss (e.g. 11% EBITDA vs 13% guidance) could compress the multiple by 30–40%. Reference: Dixon Tech trades at 38x, Kaynes at 70x — Netweb already at the high end.

## Verdict

The HIGH-conviction triggers (T1: ₹1,600 cr strategic order in execution; T4: NVIDIA Blackwell OEM moat) alone justify a ~80% top-line growth path in FY27 and 30%+ structural growth through FY29. **Adding T2, T3, T5 gives plausible FY28 revenue of ~₹3,800–4,200 cr** (a doubling from FY26 if execution holds). The **thesis is fundamentally strong**, but the entry price (108x TTM PE) demands flawless execution — small operational misses will be punished hard. Best held as an **accumulate-on-dips** stock with a 3-year horizon; not a buy-at-CMP for new positions.
