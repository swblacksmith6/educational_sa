# Netweb Technologies — Concall Analyzer (FY26 Walk-the-Talk)

**Transcripts analysed:** Q4 FY25 (5-May-25), Q1 FY26 (1-Aug-25), Q2 FY26 (3-Nov-25), Q3 FY26 (19-Jan-26), Q4 FY26 (4-May-26).

> _Explainer for a first-time reader_ — A **concall analyzer** does three things:
> 1. **Brief**: summarise what management said this quarter in plain English.
> 2. **Deep dive**: flag changes in tone, evasions, and what was NOT answered.
> 3. **Walk-the-Talk**: take what management promised in earlier calls and check whether the numbers actually came in on those promises. This is the single best test of management credibility, because it's hard to fake delivery against your own past words.

## B. Deep-dive observations across the 5 calls

### 1. The AI inflection (the dominant theme)
- AI contribution went from **5% → 14.8% → 25.4% (H1) → 48% (9M) → 43.4% (FY26)**.
- This is not a slow march — it is a step-function transition driven by (a) one ₹1,734 cr strategic order, and (b) underlying organic AI demand from enterprise + government.
- Management is **explicit that AI will settle at ~35% of mix long-term**, with HPC and Private Cloud each ~30%. Implication: AI growth rates _will_ moderate, but absolute base will be much higher.

### 2. Margin guidance has been _lowered_, not raised
- Management explicitly **revised PAT margin expectation from "around 10%" (May-25) to 9.3% (FY26 actual)**.
- EBITDA margin guidance steady at 13–14%, but FY25 actual was 14.7%; FY27 guidance is 13–14%. **Margin direction is downward, masked by volume.**
- Why? AI hardware BOM has 60–70% chip cost (NVIDIA captures most value) — Netweb is the integrator, not the IP holder.

### 3. Working capital narrative changed mid-year
- Q1 FY26: cash conversion cycle (CCC) was guided **90–110 days**.
- Q2 FY26: CCC jumped to **120 days** (advance from strategic orders not netted against debtors).
- Q3 FY26: CCC improved to **69 days** (strategic order milestone billing came in).
- Q4 FY26: CCC at **84 days** with inventory days rising to 86 (pre-stocking for IndiaAI remaining shipment).
- Net effect: **Borrowings went from ~zero to ₹270 cr** in 12 months. Management calls this "transitionary" — should reverse by Q1–Q2 FY27.

### 4. Strategic order — full chronology
- **Sep-25**: ₹450 cr AI order under IndiaAI Mission announced.
- **Nov-25 (Q2 call)**: Disclosed **₹2,184 cr total** of strategic orders. Bigger order = ₹1,734 cr.
- **Jan-26 (Q3 call)**: Executed ₹450 cr piece; strategic backlog ₹1,734 cr (= 17,336 mn). Confirmed LC-backed (no credit risk).
- **May-26 (Q4 call)**: Strategic backlog still ~₹1,600 cr; "expect to execute in next 3 quarters" (Q1–Q3 FY27). No new strategic order yet in this call.

### 5. What management was asked but didn't answer cleanly
- **Component price softness?** "I'm not supposed to guide on component pricing… AI demand is unabated, so pressure on supply chain continues."
- **Exports?** "Not focused. Will remain ~5%."
- **TPU / custom chip pivot risk?** "We can address other chips when there is significant adoption." (genuine answer, but a known risk)
- **Hyperscaler relationships?** "We are not targeting hyperscalers." This is a deliberate choice (TAM-limiting) but also means future optionality is left on the table.

### 6. Capex discipline (positive)
- Repeatedly emphasised: "We are a capability company, not a capacity company. Capacity utilisation is 65–70%."
- FY27 capex guided at ₹20–25 cr (routine only). Compare with Dixon, Kaynes deploying ₹500–1000+ cr each in capex — Netweb is asset-light.

### 7. Promoter behaviour
- **OFS of 3.65% in Feb-26** at improved liquidity reasoning. Management committed to **12-month lock-up** after the sale, so no further dilution before Feb-27.
- No pledging. No insider activity flagged.

### 8. New product launches mentioned
- **Skylus.ai** — GPU orchestration platform (Q4 FY25 launch).
- **Tyrone Camarero AI** — Blackwell-based AI training platform.
- **NVIDIA GB200 NVL4** — "Make in India" liquid-cooled AI server (manufactured locally by Netweb).
- **New 15,000 sq ft liquid-cooled SMT facility** commissioned in FY26.
- AMD Turin- and Intel Emerald Rapids-based Make-in-India servers.

## C. Walk-the-Talk scorecard (have they delivered on what they said?)

> _Explainer_ — Walk-the-Talk asks: did the actual results match the specific commitments made by management in prior calls? **HIT = delivered**, **MISS = missed**, **PARTIAL = directionally right but missed magnitude**, **PENDING = too early to judge**.

| Quarter promised | Specific commitment | What actually happened | Score |
|---|---|---|:---:|
| Q4 FY25 (May-25) | "35–40% CAGR top-line over next couple of years" | FY26 actual = **+90% YoY** | ✅ **BEAT** (helped by strategic order) |
| Q4 FY25 (May-25) | EBITDA margin 13–14% guidance | FY26 actual = **13.0%** (adj 13.3%) | ✅ **HIT** |
| Q4 FY25 (May-25) | PAT margin "around 10%" | FY26 actual = **9.3%** | 🟡 **PARTIAL** (close but below) |
| Q1 FY26 (Aug-25) | "Large AI order pipeline – we are very confident on conversion" | ₹2,184 cr strategic orders won by Q2 | ✅ **HIT** |
| Q2 FY26 (Nov-25) | Cash conversion 90–110 days | Q3 = 69d, Q4 = 84d | ✅ **BEAT** |
| Q2 FY26 (Nov-25) | "Strategic orders to be executed by FY27" — at least 1/3 in FY26 | ₹450 cr executed in Q3 FY26 (~26% of strategic order book) | ✅ **HIT** |
| Q3 FY26 (Jan-26) | "Will execute one-third of strategic order in FY26" | Executed ₹450 cr ≈ 26%; remaining ~₹1,600 cr deferred to FY27 | 🟡 **PARTIAL** (close to 1/3 but slipped) |
| Q3 FY26 (Jan-26) | "Working capital under control, no fresh borrowing needed" | Q4 borrowings jumped to ₹270 cr | 🔴 **MISS** (narrative inconsistency) |
| Q4 FY26 (May-26) | FY27: 35–40% organic growth, 13–14% margin, capex ₹20–25 cr | _Pending Q1 FY27 results_ | ⏳ **PENDING** |

**Composite walk-the-talk score: 6 HIT/BEAT + 2 PARTIAL + 1 MISS = ~78% delivery rate.** That is a strong record for a young listed company (IPO'd Jul-23). The single MISS is around working-capital borrowings — narrative said "no fresh borrowing" but Q4 borrowing climbed to ₹270 cr. Management's defence: "transitionary, LC-backed, will reverse" — must verify in Q1 FY27.

## D. Management credibility assessment

**Sanjay Lodha (CMD)** — Founder. Quote-heavy concalls, occasionally rambling but technically deep. Comfortable engaging with detail on chip architectures, component supply chain, government policy. Avoids forward guidance on component prices.

**Ankit Kumar Singhal (CFO)** — Crisp on numbers, repeated guidance ranges identically across 5 calls (consistency = good signal). Was explicit and on the record about MTM hedging treatment in Q4 FY26 (transparency = good signal).

**Sanjeev Sancheti (IR)** — Veteran IR advisor; often steps in to clarify or moderate management answers. Quality of disclosure is well above small-cap median.

**Tone trajectory across the year:** Confident → Hyper-confident (post strategic orders Q2) → Slightly defensive (Q4 on margins and borrowings) → Reaffirming (closing comments stable). No defensiveness or evasion on any major question across the 5 calls.

## E. Three biggest red flags from concalls

1. **Borrowings narrative contradicts Q3 commentary.** Management said in Q3 that strategic orders are LC-backed and no fresh borrowing is needed. In Q4, ₹270 cr of short-term debt appeared. Management explanation is plausible (RBI-mandated minimum WCDL tenure) but check Q1 FY27 — if borrowings stay above ₹150 cr through Q1, the story is fragile.
2. **Margin guidance is being managed downward.** PAT margin slipped from ~10% guidance to 9.3% delivered. Q4 FY27 onward guidance is 13–14% EBITDA, which embeds further compression vs FY23's 15.8%.
3. **Concentration in one strategic deal.** ~80% of FY26 incremental revenue traces back to a single sovereign-AI order. Any execution slip = a quarter where guidance breaks.

---

## F. Three biggest green flags from concalls

1. **Order book entering FY27 = ₹2,400 cr** (incl. ₹300 cr L1) > FY26's entire revenue. Even with zero new orders, revenue visibility is high.
2. **Asset-light model with no major capex required.** ₹20–25 cr p.a. capex with 90% revenue growth — fixed asset turnover at 33x is extraordinary.
3. **Repeat customer revenue = 81% of FY25.** This is exceptional for a hardware company and indicates sticky technical relationships.

---

**Verdict from concall lens:** **Credible management with strong delivery record but a tightening margin profile and a temporarily stretched working capital position.** Watch Q1 FY27 for (a) borrowings trajectory, (b) strategic order shipment cadence, and (c) any new strategic order from the ₹4,400 cr pipeline.
