# Creative Newtech: Concall Briefs and Walk-the-Talk Credibility Scorecard
*As of 30-Sep-2026. Built on 3 filed transcripts (Q4 FY25 call of 16-May-2025, Q2 FY26 call of 12-Nov-2025, Q4 FY26 call of 15-May-2026), 3 decks (Q3 FY25, Q3 FY26, Q1 FY27) and the FY26 annual report. All quotes are from filed transcripts, with page numbers derived from the printed "Page N of M" footers.*

## A. Concall briefs

### Q4 FY25 call (16-May-2025): new strategy, cash flow finally positive
- **Results:** FY25 total income ₹1,801.5 cr, EBITDA ₹72.9 cr (4.04%), PAT ₹53.1 cr. Honeywell brand revenue was ₹255 cr (India ₹133 cr, overseas ₹121 cr).
- **Tone:** triumphant on cash flow. The CFO said operating cash flow "*has turned positive after a long time*", at +₹21.9 cr consolidated.
- **Guidance given:** consolidated growth of "*a CAGR of 20%-25%*" over 3–4 years (p.4, MEDIUM); Honeywell growth of "*around 70%-80% every year*" (p.4, MEDIUM); working capital cut "*from 39 to 35*" days (p.7, HIGH); Honeywell brand EBITDA of "*around 15% to 17%*" (p.4).
- **Sharpest exchange:** an analyst pointed out that Honeywell grew from ₹170 cr to ₹255 cr while consolidated revenue rose only about ₹23 cr, so distribution must have shrunk. Management conceded the distribution business was "*very flat*" because of price erosion and no new brands.

### Q2 FY26 call (12-Nov-2025): distribution restarts, receivables double
- **Results:** H1 FY26 total income ₹1,056.8 cr, EBITDA ₹41.8 cr, PAT ₹29.1 cr. H1 Honeywell revenue ≈ ₹148 cr (India ₹65 cr, overseas ₹82 cr).
- **New channels:** Matrix and Sparsh surveillance distribution, plus a data-centre cabling push.
- **Receivables:** standalone receivables rose from ₹208 cr to ₹440 cr. Working-capital days went from 51 to 58 (p.7), which contradicts the May promise of 35.
- **Guidance given:**
  - Honeywell FY26: "*pessimistically we can say we will easily cross INR365 crores - INR370 crores*" (p.5, HIGH).
  - Brand EBITDA "*we hope to grow it to 21%*" (p.9, LOW).
  - PAT margin "*around 4.5% to 5%*" in 1–2 years (p.10, LOW).
  - Own brand in "*the first quarter of February 26*" (p.19, MEDIUM).
  - FY26 PAT of ₹60 cr: an **analyst's number**, which management confirmed: "*It looks like we will end up there or slightly better*" (p.21, MEDIUM).
- **Sharpest exchange (p.12–13):** an analyst derived that the ex-brand business earns about 0.2% EBITDA. The CFO agreed ("*my EBITDA margin is 0.2%*"), then moved to standalone figures.
- **Uninterpretable guidance:** the CFO's FY26 EBITDA estimate of "*INR62 crores to INR63 crores*" (p.12) conflicts with the ₹41.8 cr already booked in H1 and the ₹104 cr actually reported. It is **not scored**.

### Q4 FY26 call (15-May-2026): new CFO, record numbers, cash silence
- **Results:** FY26 revenue ₹2,699.8 cr (+51%), EBITDA ₹104 cr, PAT ₹70.29 cr, which includes ₹8.42 cr of minority interest (not mentioned on the call). Brand revenue ₹369.6 cr (India ₹232.0 cr, overseas ₹137.6 cr).
- **The new CFO (Ajit Thakur) did not mention operating cash flow** (−₹271.9 cr) in his prepared remarks. Receivables (₹565 cr) and debt came up only when analysts asked.
- **Guidance given:**
  - Consolidated growth "*at least in a year by 25%, 30%*" (p.8, MEDIUM).
  - Honeywell "*grow at least by 50%*" (p.25, MEDIUM) and brand business "*50% to 60% every year*" (p.21, LOW: "our aspiration").
  - Receivables back to "*the normal 13%, 14%*" of turnover (p.26, MEDIUM).
  - The Middle East could fall to "*only 50% of our business*" there (p.28), a volunteered negative.
- **Brand margin:** now "*EBITDA of 13%*" (p.14), down from 15–17% guided a year earlier. The CMD added "*there is no tax*" because the business sits in Hong Kong (p.14).

## C. Pattern analysis

**The split is clean.**
- **Everything that is signing or booking was delivered:** new distribution agreements, the own-brand launch (late), revenue growth and reported PAT.
- **Everything that is cash or margin was missed:** working-capital days, operating cash flow, brand EBITDA margin, and Honeywell's growth rate.

This team is good at adding revenue and poor at collecting it. Revenue guidance is therefore more believable than any guidance about margins, receivables or cash.

**Named patterns**
- **Restating their own guidance.** In May-26 the CMD said "*Our guidance was INR359 crores, INR360 crores. We did INR370 crores*" (p.15). The Nov-25 guidance was ₹365–370 cr, described as the *pessimistic* case (p.5). Lowering the bar after the fact is the most concerning item in this scorecard.
- **Definitional drift.** Segments moved from EB / FMSG / FMCT / FMEG to Brand / Market Entry in FY26. FY25 "FMSG" was ₹385.4 cr; restated "Brand" FY25 is ₹270.7 cr.
- **Moving dates.** The Honeywell renewal date moved twice (Dec-2025 → end-2027 → Mar-2027). The 50/50 target moved from 2029 to 2030.
- **Deck ahead of the facts.** The Aug-26 deck lists the "Acquisition of Infinova India" and calls SCL "wholly owned". The annual report, approved at the same 4-Aug board meeting, says no binding Infinova agreement exists, and AOC-1 says SCL is 77.5% owned.
- **No pre-warning on cash.** The May-25 promise of positive operating cash flow was never withdrawn. By Nov-25, receivables had doubled; management framed it as "September month" timing (p.7–8).
- **Profit headline includes minorities.** "PAT ₹70.29 cr" is used on the call, in the deck and in the Chairman's letter. Owners' share is ₹61.9 cr.

## D. Where management was straight
- In Nov-25 the CFO conceded that ex-brand EBITDA is about 0.2% (p.13). That is unusually candid.
- In May-26 the CMD volunteered that the Middle East could fall to about 50% of normal (p.28), and that "*we have to do a better job in the debtor receivable days*" (p.26).
- The CMD disclosed the zero-tax status of SCL on the call (p.14).
- In May-25 management admitted the distribution business was flat and that no new brands were added.

This looks like **candid but optimistic forecasting on operations, with loose presentation of headline facts**. That is not concealment, but it means the decks cannot be relied on without checking them against the annual report.

## E. How to use this rating
1. **Revenue guidance** of 25–30% should be underwritten at about 20%. **Margin, cash and receivable guidance should get no credit** until one clean half-year is delivered.
2. **Use owners' PAT (₹61.9 cr) and EPS, not the ₹70.3 cr headline.** Use operating cash flow and receivable days as the primary health metrics; brand revenue growth comes second.
3. **Re-test dates:**
   - Q2 FY27 results (Nov-2026): receivables should be at or below ~₹450 cr, with positive H1 operating cash flow.
   - Honeywell renewal announcement (by Mar-2027).
   - FY27 annual report: the Click Retail balance and SCL ownership.
   - Hitting the first two would lift the rating to MEDIUM.
