# Creative Newtech: Growth Triggers 1-Pager
*As of 30-Sep-2026. CMP ₹1,104 (29-Sep-2026, Bajaj Finserv). Mcap ≈ ₹1,658 cr. Consolidated.*
*FY26 revenue ₹2,704.6 cr. PAT ₹70.3 cr (owners ₹61.9 cr). Q1 FY27 revenue ₹476.1 cr, PAT ₹13.5 cr. Walk-the-Talk credibility: LOW–MEDIUM; management-sourced amounts are discounted accordingly.*

## Business in one line
An Indian technology distributor (86% of revenue at 2–4% margin) with a Hong Kong-held Honeywell licence business (14% of revenue at ~33% segment margin before group overheads). It is shifting from consumer IT into government, surveillance and telecom-infrastructure projects that pay slowly.

## The triggers

### 1. BharatNet Odisha (BSNL Middle-Mile) — 🟡 MEDIUM on existence, ⚪ on size and timing
- **What:** an Advance Work Order from BSNL for the Odisha circle, with total project value about ₹3,194.83 cr (Q1 FY27 deck). CRISIL (Aug-26) confirms CNL as Project Implementation Agency and expects execution plus long-term maintenance income.
- **Why only MEDIUM:** this is an *advance* work order, not a final contract. CNL's share, scope, consortium structure, phasing, margin and bank-guarantee needs are **not disclosed**. The headline value is more than the whole FY26 revenue base. CRISIL itself flags that such projects will keep receivables high.
- **Quantum:** not estimable from filings. Illustratively, if CNL executes ₹300–500 cr a year at distribution-like 3–4% EBITDA, that adds ₹9–20 cr of EBITDA *(analyst illustration, not guidance)*, with a matching receivable build.
- **Upgrade if:** the definitive agreement and CNL's scope are disclosed.

### 2. Surveillance / Make-in-India distribution — 🟢 HIGH that it exists, 🟡 on quality
- **What:** Matrix, Sparsh, Dahua, EIZO, Kaspersky, PDRL; plus FCI Smart Warehousing (₹35.89 cr order, Q1 FY27 deck).
- **Evidence:** India revenue rose from ₹678 cr to ₹1,797 cr (AR26 note 23B). Q1 FY27 standalone EBITDA margin was 4.46% vs 2.85% (Q1 FY27 deck), and the CMD attributes higher margins to Make-in-India vendors (May-26 call).
- **Why HIGH on existence but only MEDIUM on quality:** the revenue is already booked, but it came with extended credit (receivable days 49 → 76). The trigger is only worth something if it converts to cash.
- **Quantum:** already largely in the FY26 base. Incremental FY27 value is in margin (Q1 standalone EBITDA +160 bps).

### 3. Honeywell brand growth + WOZOYO own brand — 🟡 MEDIUM (Honeywell) / ⚪ OPTIONALITY (WOZOYO)
- **Evidence:** FY26 brand revenue ₹369.6 cr (May-26 call). Guidance: Honeywell "*grow at least by 50%*" (May-26, p.25). WOZOYO has launched in India and the US; ₹8 cr of launch spend was expensed in Q1 FY27.
- **Against:** Q1 FY27 brand revenue was only +8.6% YoY. The Middle East (~50% of overseas brand sales) is disrupted. Brand EBITDA has been guided down to 13%.
- **Quantum:** at a credibility-adjusted +20–25% (not 50%), brand revenue is about ₹445–460 cr in FY27, +₹75–90 cr. At about 13% EBITDA that adds ₹10–12 cr, 22.5% of which belongs to SCL's minority. WOZOYO: management says ₹25–30 cr in a full year (May-26, p.25). Treated as optionality.

### 4. Brand demerger — ⚪ OPTIONALITY
- The CMD said: "*for the next 15 months, there is no plan*", then a 12–15 month process, and only once the brand business reaches about ₹1,000 cr (May-26 call). That is realistically FY29 or later.
- This would be a re-rating trigger, not an earnings one. It **cannot be a reason to own the stock now.**

### 5. Operating leverage on interest and overheads — ⚪ OPTIONALITY
- The CMD's targets (May-26): manpower 2–2.5%, logistics ~1%, admin under 1%, finance cost under 1% of revenue; PAT margin 4.5–5% in 2–3 years (Nov-25, p.10).
- Actual: FY26 PAT margin was 2.6%, and Q1 FY27 interest cost doubled YoY. **The trend points the other way.**

## Explicitly NOT counted as triggers
- **Market-size slides** (Indian surveillance $12.25 bn by 2030; data centres $21.8 bn; India AI market $325 bn by 2033). These are sector platitudes with no contract attached.
- **Infinova India acquisition.** The deck lists it as done; the annual report says no binding agreement has been signed. No price is disclosed.
- **"2 to 3 brands above ₹1,000 cr"** and **50/50 brand mix by 2029/2030.** These are aspirations; the target date has already slipped once and brand share is falling.
- **Honeywell licence expansion to more categories.** Management said this happens only if Honeywell offers it.

## What can break the thesis
1. **Cash:** receivables stay above ~20% of sales and debt keeps funding growth (CRISIL downgrade trigger: interest cover below 3×).
2. **Honeywell:** the renewal due Mar-2027 lands in the middle of Honeywell's corporate split. The brand segment is ~59% of consolidated segment results (₹126.3 cr of ₹213.6 cr).
3. **The Middle East plus memory/CPU cost inflation (+30–40% per the CMD)** squeezes the brand margin further.

## Verdict
The HIGH-conviction trigger (surveillance / Make-in-India) is **already in the numbers, and it is the thing consuming the cash**. Everything that would re-rate the stock is MEDIUM or OPTIONALITY:
- BharatNet scope,
- Honeywell at 50% growth,
- WOZOYO,
- the demerger.

The evidence supports "buy the evidence, not the plan", and the evidence to wait for is cash conversion, not another contract announcement.
