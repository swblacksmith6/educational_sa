## H. Final Take-aways

1. **Core business is real and improving in quality, but lumpy.** Halol is winning marquee defence / railway / industrial orders against global incumbents (ABB-replacement claim). Revenue trajectory is positive in trend but volatile in any single quarter.

2. **The SiC story is real but is at least 6-12 months behind the originally communicated schedule** — and the steady-state revenue potential has been **halved** (₹1,200 → ₹600 Cr) within a six-month window. Investors who anchored to the May-25 numbers should re-anchor.

3. **The new MD (Ramesh Kumar) is the single most positive operational change in 9 months.** His CG Power experience (steering a fraud-hit company through NCLT avoidance) gives RIR a turnaround-capable hand. Watch the next 1-2 calls for whether he restates guidance with firmer numbers.

4. **Funding gap is the gating risk.** Phase-1 ₹70 Cr debt should close by mid-March 2026 (Q4 FY26); Phase-2 (₹395-400 Cr, ~50 % govt) has no debt or equity tied. Expect a meaningful equity raise in FY27 — at the current share price, this could be sized at ₹150-200 Cr. Dilution risk: real.

5. **Walk-the-Talk Rating: LOW-MEDIUM credibility.** Management is honest in restating; not honest in original sizing. Treat all forward numerics with a 20-30 % haircut and a 6-month delay assumption until proven otherwise.
