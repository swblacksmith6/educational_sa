# RIR Power Electronics Ltd — Growth Triggers 1-Pager
*As of 16-May-2026 | BSE: 517035 | CMP: pull from Screener.in (post 1:1 bonus + 1:5 split, FV ₹2) | Source: Screener.in*

> **Valuation discipline:** This is a 1-pager for trigger mapping, not a valuation note. CMP / Mcap / TTM P/E must be sourced from Screener.in immediately before any position-sizing decision — they have moved sharply on SiC narrative since FY25 close and the script changed after corporate actions.

## Business in one line
India's only listed pure-play **power-semiconductor + power-equipment** maker (Halol, Gujarat); now executing a **₹618 Cr Silicon Carbide ecosystem (EPI + Device-fab + Packaging) in Odisha** — first-of-its-kind in India — for railways, defence, renewables, EV-power, and green-hydrogen end-markets.

---

## The 6 Growth Triggers

### Trigger 1: Odisha SiC Phase-1 (EPI wafer line) commercial production — **MEDIUM**
- **What:** 150 mm SiC epitaxial-wafer line (4,000 wafers/month at full ramp); two reactors — one delivered, second arriving ~mid-March-2026; "we are not growing the boule, we are buying SiC substrate wafers and growing 20-180 micron defect-free EPI on top" (Mehta, Q2 FY26 p.6).
- **Evidence:** "Once we get the power, we will have the commercial production started at Bhubaneswar facility … within 90 days." (Mehta, Q3 FY26 call p.6). Clean room ready by "end of February or mid-March" 2026 (Mehta, Q3 FY26 p.8). State subsidy ₹52 Cr already received in two tranches (Ankit Shah, Q3 FY26 p.7).
- **Quantum:** ₹60 Cr revenue in FY27 from EPI alone (Mehta, Q2 FY26 p.6); 25-28 % EBITDA margin target (Mehta, Q4 FY25 p.6).
- **Timeline:** First EPI revenue in Q1-Q2 FY27 (slipped from Q4 FY26 guided in May-25); meaningful ramp from H2 FY27.
- **Why MEDIUM (not HIGH):** Power 33 kV connection still pending from Odisha state utility; reactor-2 still en route; commercial qualification = 60 days post first-power. Customer LOIs in place (Alpha Omega Semi, ST Micro, Microchip per Mehta, Q2 FY26 p.7) but no firm signed offtake contract disclosed.

### Trigger 2: Halol industrial-product wins — defence & infrastructure replacement of global incumbents — **HIGH**
- **What:** Halol plant has delivered/is on the verge of delivering several large, irreplaceable industrial orders against ABB / Siemens / Mitsubishi: (i) 10 MW base-power rectifier for rocket launcher (tested in Q3 FY26); (ii) 10 kV / 200 kA capacitor discharge switch for Indian Army nearing completion; (iii) OSD-595 (3,600 V) and OSD-550 (4,300 V) for diesel + electric locomotives — RDSO Part-I supplier; (iv) sub-cycle transfer / fault-current-limiter assemblies for grid; (v) 22 kV SiC diode delivered to **US Army** historically.
- **Evidence:** "We provided and we tested almost 10 megawatt rectifier for base power for rocket launcher … high voltage capacitor discharge switch for Indian Army, which is about 10,000 volts and 200,000 amps … we are by the way replacing ABB from this applications" (Mehta, Q3 FY26 p.3). RDSO Part-I status verified in AR25 p.6.
- **Quantum:** Halol revenue ran-rate ~₹85-90 Cr FY25 → guided ₹100-110 Cr FY26 by management; under new MD Ramesh Kumar (joined Feb-26) we expect ₹130-160 Cr ran-rate by FY28 based on the order pipeline and industry experience. EBITDA margin 14-17 % achievable (proved in Q2 FY26).
- **Timeline:** Continuous flow; orders book over FY26-FY27.
- **Why HIGH:** Products are already at advanced testing / RDSO-approved stage with named PSU/defence customers. India has zero domestic alternative on most of these — switching cost to ABB-equivalent is years.

### Trigger 3: First international commercial SiC shipment & global distribution via Richardson Electronics — **MEDIUM**
- **What:** First $20,000 international shipment of SiC product was made in Q3 FY26 to Richardson Electronics USA (one of the largest power-semi distributors globally). Volume orders expected post-evaluation.
- **Evidence:** "Last quarter, we did our first international shipment to a very reputed global dealer and there was a sample order of $20,000. Once that is evaluated, we expect volume orders from Richardson Electronics which is the one of the largest and the most reputed distributor." (Mehta, Q3 FY26 p.3). Indian Navy 10,000-device SiC shipment ($95K) executed in Q2 FY26 via Taiwan contract manufacturer with RIR IP.
- **Quantum:** Management guides **65 % of SiC Phase-1 revenue will be export** (Mehta, Q2 FY26 p.6). If Phase-1 ramps to ~₹60 Cr in FY27, export contribution ≈ ₹40 Cr; could scale to ₹100-200 Cr by FY29 if Richardson channel develops.
- **Timeline:** Volume orders contingent on $20K-batch evaluation — typically 3-6 months. Real revenue impact FY27 onwards.
- **Why MEDIUM:** Distributor relationship is real and the first shipment has happened, but no firm volume contract size has been disclosed.

### Trigger 4: India's green-hydrogen electrolyser rectifier opportunity — **MEDIUM**
- **What:** RIR is one of very few Indian players supplying high-power DC rectifiers (60 V/2,000 A; 200 V/3,200 A; 12 V/10,000 A) for water electrolysers used in green hydrogen, hydrogenation, electro-chlorination, and refinery applications.
- **Evidence:** Already commissioned at 15 MW Kadodiya/Ujjain green hydrogen facility (AR25, Investor Presentation Jun-24 p.7). AR25 highlights "rectifiers integral to electrolysis applications" (p.5). India's NGHM (National Green Hydrogen Mission) targets 5 MMT of green H₂ capacity by 2030 — implies tens-of-GW of electrolyser capacity.
- **Quantum:** Specific revenue per electrolyser MW: ~₹40-60 L of rectifier value. If RIR captures 5-10 % of incremental Indian electrolyser capacity over FY27-FY30, opportunity = ₹150-300 Cr cumulative.
- **Timeline:** Order flow already starting; meaningful revenue contribution from FY27.
- **Why MEDIUM:** Demand is real, RIR is already credentialled, but final L-1/L-2 wins on specific RFP-based electrolyser tenders haven't been disclosed.

### Trigger 5: New MD Mr. N. Ramesh Kumar — execution & guidance reset — **MEDIUM**
- **What:** Mr. N. Ramesh Kumar (ex-CG Power business head; led CG out of NCLT-risk during the 2019 fraud disclosure; ex-Diffusion Engineers Ltd) joined as MD & CEO on 11-Feb-2026. Stated priorities: external — sales reorg, brand-building, customer-connect, new applications/products; internal — operational excellence, IT, Odisha oversight.
- **Evidence:** "I am pleased to welcome our new MD and CEO, Ramesh Kumar ji" (Mehta, Q3 FY26 p.3); "I have taken three targets for internally and three targets externally" (Ramesh Kumar, Q3 FY26 p.5).
- **Quantum:** Hard to size — but the absence of an India-resident MD for the prior 12+ months was repeatedly cited by analysts as a credibility gap; resolution of this gap could unlock institutional ownership and re-rate the stock.
- **Timeline:** Tangible operational impact from H2 FY27 onwards; firm forward numerics likely from Q1 FY27 concall (May-2026).
- **Why MEDIUM:** New incumbent with strong track record but only 4 days on the job at the time of latest call. Watch the next 1-2 quarters.

### Trigger 6: NSE listing + potential strategic investor — re-rating trigger — **OPTIONALITY**
- **What:** RIR is currently BSE-only (scrip 517035). NSE listing process is underway; expected by 31-Mar-2026. Management has separately confirmed it is in discussions with **strategic investors and FIIs** ("we are in talks with strategic partners to join in but we will not be able to share any names currently" — Ankit Shah, Q2 FY26 p.6). ISM-2 bid (Indian Semiconductor Mission Phase-2) is also being prepared with potential foreign tech partner (Mitsubishi / Ukrainian company) for medium-and-high voltage IGBTs.
- **Evidence:** Q2 FY26 + Q3 FY26 concalls.
- **Quantum:** A confirmed strategic investor with a name (e.g., a global power-semi major or a sovereign-fund) at a 5-10 % stake would typically re-rate Indian semi names by 20-40 %. NSE inclusion expands the institutional buyer base meaningfully.
- **Timeline:** NSE listing — 3-6 months (next quarter). Strategic investor — open-ended; ISM-2 — depends on government tender cycle.
- **Why OPTIONALITY:** No name has been disclosed; **this cannot be the main reason to own the stock**. Pure upside if it lands.

## What can break the thesis (top 3 risks)

1. **Odisha execution slippage / cost over-run.** Phase-1 timeline has already slipped 6 months and full-capacity revenue guidance was halved (₹1,200 → ₹600 Cr) within six months. If Phase-2 (₹400 Cr packaging + device-fab) is delayed by another 6-12 months or the asset turnover proves below the guided 0.8-1.0×, the SiC thesis weakens materially. The ₹42 Cr SiC IP (intangible on the balance sheet) would face impairment risk.

2. **Funding gap → equity dilution.** Phase-1 ₹70 Cr debt is at in-principle approval (closure expected mid-March-2026). **Phase-2 financing (~₹400 Cr) is not yet tied** — Ankit Shah explicitly said "we'll tie up Phase-2 once Phase-1 is done" (Q3 FY26 p.10). At current Mcap, a Phase-2 equity raise could be 5-15 % dilutive. The recent track-record of warrant conversions and treasury-share monetisation makes further dilution likely.

3. **Governance & related-party density.** FY25 saw a ₹42 Cr intangible purchase from related-party SiCamore Semiconductor (USA); FY26 sees sale of 100 % subsidiary Visicon Power Electronics to related-party Silicon Power Corp (USA) at undisclosed terms; 7.5 pp promoter shareholding reduction; multiple long-tenured director exits. None individually is disqualifying — but the cluster matters. Any FY26 RPT escalation (e.g., further IP purchases, royalty arrangements, additional asset transfers) would damage the discount-to-fair-value the stock should command.

## Verdict

The **HIGH-conviction Halol industrial-product trigger (Trigger 2) alone underwrites a fair-value floor at current revenue + modest growth**, with the MEDIUM-conviction Odisha SiC trigger (Trigger 1) providing the asymmetric upside that has driven the stock's narrative. **The combined HIGH + MEDIUM triggers can plausibly take revenue to ₹350-450 Cr by FY29 at a 17-20 % EBITDA margin**, even if the full ₹600 Cr Odisha-only guidance proves out only by FY31 — i.e. the thesis works even with management's downward-revised numbers. **The OPTIONALITY triggers (NSE listing, strategic investor, ISM-2) are the kicker, not the case.** Bet sizing should reflect that this is a small-cap with a sub-1× CFO/PAT history, an aggressive related-party-funded capex programme, and a credibility hole that the new MD's first two quarters need to close.
