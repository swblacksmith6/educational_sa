1.5 Bear Case

  1. Massive related-party intangible. ₹42 Cr to promoter-controlled SiCamore for SiC IP. If Odisha slips or under-utilises, the intangible must be impaired — and minority shareholders foot the bill while promoter group has already received the cash.
  2. CFO has not supported PAT for three years. 3-yr CFO/PAT ≈ 0.12. If Odisha working-capital build adds to this, repeated dilution becomes the funding path.
  3. Single-segment, opaque disclosure. No sub-segment mix, no top-customer share, no order book, no SiC unit economics in the AR. Concalls are light on hard numbers.
  4. Tiny company taking on outsized capex. ₹618 Cr SiC fab vs FY25 revenue ₹86 Cr (~7×). RIR has no prior wafer-fab experience; SiC fabs are notoriously yield-sensitive.
  5. Governance optics. 7.5 pp promoter sell-down, multiple long-tenured directors out, SiC IP from common-control entity, Visicon sold to common-control entity at undisclosed terms.

3.1 Top 3 Thesis-Breakers

  1. Odisha execution slippage / cost over-run. Phase-1 has already slipped 6 months and full-capacity guidance was halved within 6 months. If Phase-2 (₹400 Cr) slips another 6-12 months or asset turnover under-delivers vs. 0.8-1.0×, the SiC thesis weakens materially — and the ₹42 Cr SiC IP intangible faces impairment.
  2. Funding gap → equity dilution. Phase-2 (~₹400 Cr) financing is not yet tied (Ankit Shah Q3 FY26 p.10). At current Mcap, an equity raise could be 5-15% dilutive. The recent track of warrant conversions and treasury monetisation makes further dilution likely.
  3. Governance & related-party density. FY25 had ₹70 Cr in RPT vs ₹86 Cr revenue (mostly the SiC IP). Visicon sale to common-control Silicon Power Corp at undisclosed terms is pending. Any FY26 RPT escalation damages the discount.

4. Verdict

WATCH — revisit after FY26 results + Odisha milestone updates. The HIGH-conviction Halol industrial trigger underwrites a fair-value floor at current revenue + modest growth; the MEDIUM Odisha trigger provides the asymmetric upside. Combined HIGH + MEDIUM triggers can plausibly take revenue to ₹350-450 Cr by FY29 at 17-20% EBITDA margin, even on management's downward-revised numbers.

Convert to BUY when (a) CFO/PAT normalises above 0.7 over a rolling 4-quarter period, (b) Odisha Phase-1 EPI shows ≥₹15 Cr of actual revenue in any quarter, and (c) Phase-2 financing is closed without >10% equity dilution. Convert to PASS if related-party flows escalate, Phase-2 slips beyond FY28, or the SiC IP intangible takes an impairment charge.
