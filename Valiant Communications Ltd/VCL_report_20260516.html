Final Verdict

🟡 WATCH — re-rate to BUY-WORTHY on confirmation of working-capital reversal in H1 FY26 numbers (expected Nov-2025).

Valiant is a credible, debt-free, cash-positive, Make-in-India electronics manufacturer with genuine technology depth (PMU, IEEE C37.94, GPS+NavIC, EMP-hardened NAS), a 3.5× revenue gross-order-book, NSCS "Trusted Source" status, accelerating EBITDA margins (24.5%→31.1%→37%), and a Walk-the-Talk record of 6/6 delivered forward statements. The reasons to wait rather than pass: (a) FY25 cash quality was poor (CFO/PAT = 0.16), (b) management has staked credibility on a reversal that the next reported balance sheet will either confirm or contradict, and (c) the stock embeds high expectations. Buy only after Q2 FY26 numbers confirm receivable days < 100 and ex-WC CFO/PAT > 0.7; until then, this is a high-conviction WATCH.
