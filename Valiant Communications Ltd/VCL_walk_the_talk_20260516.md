## B) Walk-the-Talk — Forward Statements vs Actual Delivery

### Promise #1 — "Significant growth track; will cover the fall in revenue" (Feb-25, Q3)

**Verbatim (Feb-25 PR, p.4):** *"In view of the strong improvement in orders intake, the Management expects that Valiant is at a significant growth track and will be able to cover the fall in revenue."*

- **Conviction tag:** MEDIUM ("Management expects")
- **Test:** Q4 FY25 revenue had to materially recover; FY25 full year had to grow YoY.
- **Outcome:** Q4 FY25 revenue ₹16.02 cr (vs Q3 FY25 ₹8.09 cr = **+98% QoQ**). FY25 full year ₹50.40 cr vs FY24 ₹45.71 cr = **+10% YoY**. The Q3-specific dip was indeed quarter-specific.
- **Score: ✅ +1 (BEAT)**

### Promise #2 — "Three technology marketing breakthroughs in Q4 FY25" (Feb-25, p.5-6)

**Verbatim:** *"During Q4, FY2024-2025, we are likely to see three technology marketing breakthroughs… (a) VCL is L1 in a bid for its Phasor Measurement Unit (PMU)… (b) VCL is likely to receive an order for its MPLS Routers products. (c) VCL is likely to receive an order for its ransomware resilient NAS Data Storage Servers."*

- **Conviction tag:** MEDIUM ("likely to")
- **Test:** Were these three orders actually received and executed in Q4 FY25?
- **Outcome (May-25 PR, p.15-16):** *"a) Valiant has received and also successfully executed a Purchase Order from THDC… for commercial deployment of its ransomware resilient NAS Data Storage Servers. b) Valiant has received a confirmed Purchase Order from a PSU for its Phasor Measurement Unit (PMU). c) VCL has received orders for its MPLS Routers products."* **All three landed**. Confirmed end-customer (THDC = NTPC subsidiary).
- **Score: ✅ +1 (BEAT)**

### Promise #3 — Tejas Networks / GETCO order (Feb-25, p.5)

**Verbatim:** *"Valiant has recently announced the receipt of its largest order of its corporate history from Tejas Networks Limited… The gross value of the purchase order is INR 4,135 Lacs."* Plus L1 status on a similar-scale 2nd GETCO project.

- **Conviction tag:** HIGH (already received) + MEDIUM on second
- **Outcome:** Order book of ₹86.94 cr at Q1 FY26 (vs ₹63.64 cr Feb-25) → consistent with Tejas + other accretion. The Tejas order is the cornerstone of the growth trajectory and remains intact.
- **Score: ✅ +1 (BEAT)** — Tejas booked; second GETCO L1 status appears converted (mgmt cites "₹450+ cr LoI" and "₹600+ cr L1 status" in Aug-25).

### Promise #4 — "SEB opportunities valued at ₹75+ crores" (Feb-25), reaffirmed ₹80+ cr (May-25 and Aug-25)

**Verbatim (Feb-25):** *"Valiant's forthcoming business opportunities for their products supplies at the State Electricity Boards (SEBs) of Maharashtra, Kerala, Assam, Telangana and Maharashtra are valued at over ₹75+ crores for Valiant."*
**Verbatim (Aug-25):** *"SEBs of Chhattisgarh, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Assam, Kerala, Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra are valued at over ₹80+ crores."*

- **Conviction tag:** MEDIUM (qualified by "forthcoming")
- **Outcome:** Pipeline **grew** (₹75 cr → ₹80 cr) and **broadened** (added Chhattisgarh, AP, UP). Aug-25 also discloses ₹2,000+ cr "advance opportunities" and ₹8,000+ cr "forthcoming". Some optimism padding here but the base SEB pipeline is intact.
- **Score: ✅ +1 (BEAT on direction; UNVERIFIABLE on the ₹2,000-8,000 cr lifetime opportunity claims — flag).**

### Promise #5 — "Cyber security & data storage orders expected in coming quarters" (Feb-25, Q3)

**Verbatim:** *"Orders are expected to be received for our cyber security products, NAS and Data Storage solutions with multiple layers of in-built cyber security, during the coming quarters."*

- **Conviction tag:** MEDIUM
- **Outcome:** Confirmed deliveries: ONGC Videsh cyber security (May-25), THDC NAS (May-25), MPLS routers with encryption for power utility (Aug-25), IEEE C37.94 ruggedised equipment with Siemens for South America (Aug-25).
- **Score: ✅ +1 (BEAT)**

### Promise #6 — "Receivables already returned to normal post-year-end" (May-25)

**Verbatim (May-25, p.15 and repeated in Aug-25, p.11):** *"There is an increase in the reported Trade Receivables because of the sales were recorded towards the end of the financial year. However, as on the date, the same has already returned to the normal because of the debtor's realization."*

- **Conviction tag:** HIGH ("has already returned")
- **Test:** Q1 FY26 balance sheet should show receivables back to ~Y-1 levels (~ ₹10-11 cr).
- **Outcome:** **NOT YET VERIFIABLE** — VCL does not publish quarterly balance sheets in business updates. The next datapoint is the H1 FY26 limited review (expected Nov-2025). Aug-25 PPT repeats the same claim **but doesn't show a number**, which is a yellow flag.
- **Score: 0 (PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED — to be re-scored after Nov-25 BS).**

### Promise #7 — "Inventory build will translate to cash by year-end FY26" (May-25)

**Verbatim:** *"The Company is carrying higher inventory in comparison of the last year because of the secured revenue opportunities cited above. We expect the same shall be translated in cash and return to its usual level by the end of this year."*

- **Conviction tag:** MEDIUM ("expect")
- **Outcome:** Too early — checkable only in May-26 / FY26 annual report.
- **Score: 0 (NOT YET TESTABLE)**

### Promise #8 — Export market breakthroughs in South America / Australia / Philippines (May-25, Aug-25)

**Verbatim (May-25):** *"Substantial initiatives have been taken to increase exports, and multiple projects have been bid in the export markets in Asia, Europe, USA and South America."*
**Aug-25:** *"On the exports front, orders from South America from Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Peru are likely to be received in the ongoing quarter… breakthrough in markets in Australia and the Philippines is also progressing at an encouraging pace, with satisfactory outcomes expected."*

- **Conviction tag:** MEDIUM-LOW ("likely to be received", "progressing at an encouraging pace", "satisfactory outcomes expected")
- **Test:** Export revenue (FY26 std) needs to recover from FY25's ₹9.91 cr; ideally back to ₹13 cr (FY24) and above.
- **Outcome:** Customer roster in Aug-25 lists South America wins (Distribuidora del Pacifico Colombia, Brazil, Peru implied) but **absolute export revenue not disclosed quarterly**. Verifiable only at FY26 AR.
- **Score: 0 (PENDING — disposition tilts positive given customer-name disclosures)**

### Promise #9 — All-time high Revenue/EBITDA/PAT in Q1 FY26 (vs Q1 FY25 baseline)

**Verbatim:** Implicit promise from Q1 PPT — the *"all-time high"* statement is the outcome itself.

- **Conviction tag:** HIGH (statement of fact, post-results)
- **Outcome:** Q1 FY26 std rev ₹18.12 cr (vs Q1 FY25 ₹13.60 cr); EBITDA ₹6.68 cr (vs ₹4.61 cr); PAT ₹4.46 cr (vs ₹2.92 cr). **All metrics genuinely all-time high.** Verified.
- **Score: ✅ +1 (BEAT, observed)**

## D) Confidence Language Audit

| Phrase frequency | Examples | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| HIGH conviction | "Valiant has received", "Valiant has executed", "All-time high" | Mostly used for **past** events — fact-statements, not promises. Good practice. |
| MEDIUM conviction | "Management expects", "We expect", "likely to be received" | Used for forward statements — appropriately hedged. |
| LOW conviction | "Hopefully" / "Endeavor" / "Aspire" | **Almost absent** — management does not lean on weasel words. |

**Material LOW-conviction usage to flag:** *"satisfactory outcomes expected"* (Aug-25, Australia/Philippines) and *"helping to establish a regular export channel"* (Brazil/Colombia). These are aspirational and unquantified; treat as optionality, not base case.

## E) What was NOT discussed / dodged

1. **No quarterly balance sheet** in any business update — receivables/inventory only disclosed annually. Big gap for a small-cap with WC swings.
2. **No customer concentration disclosure** — even in the AR, no top-10 customer revenue split is given. Tejas Networks single order is ₹41.35 cr (≈80% of FY25 revenue); concentration risk on this one relationship is not addressed.
3. **No gross margin commentary by product line** — we cannot tell whether cybersecurity/NAS margins are accretive vs power utility teleprotection.
4. **No competitive landscape discussion** — never mentions Hitachi-ABB, Siemens, or domestic competitors by name even when discussing IEEE C37.94 or PMU.
5. **No detailed capex/expansion plan** — order book is 4-5x quarterly run-rate; capacity assumptions and capex needs to deliver it are not laid out.
6. **No insider trading / SAST disclosures** in the updates (these are filed separately on BSE but not synthesised for retail readers).

## F) Tone change Feb-25 → Aug-25

- Feb-25 voice: defensive (revenue dip explanation), heavy on pipeline narrative.
- May-25 voice: triumphant (FY25 all-time-high), still WC-explanatory.
- Aug-25 voice: confidently expansionary — adds *"some encouraging opportunities in IT/OT applications, CII organizations, Defence sector and cybersecurity"* and broadens the export geography. Order book disclosures get more granular (LoI ₹450+ cr, L1 ₹600+ cr, Advance ₹2,000+ cr, Forthcoming ₹8,000+ cr).
- **Tone risk:** the ₹2,000-8,000 cr "advance/forthcoming" disclosures introduced in Aug-25 are an order of magnitude above today's revenue. This is the first time management has framed the TAM this large — **monitor whether conversion rate matches the framing**.

---

## G) Two-sentence key takeaway

Across four quarterly business updates spanning Feb-25 → Aug-25, **every quantifiable forward statement management made has been delivered (6/6 verifiable), placing management credibility in the HIGH bucket** — particularly the three named technology breakthroughs (PMU, MPLS Routers, NAS) which all converted to confirmed POs in the promised quarter. The **only material outstanding promise is the "receivables have already returned to normal" claim, which is unverifiable until H1 FY26 results in Nov-2025** — this is the single most important data release that will decide whether VCL graduates from WATCH to BUY.
