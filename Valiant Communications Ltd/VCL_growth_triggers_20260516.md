# Valiant Communications (VCL) — Growth Triggers 1-Pager
*As of 16-May-2026 | BSE: 526775 | CMP: not verified — please cross-check Screener.in before sizing | Mcap reference: ₹297.95 cr as of 31-Mar-2025 per company IP, p.7 (Apr-25 PPT)*

## Business in one line
A Delhi-based, debt-free, Make-in-India manufacturer of mission-critical communication, transmission, teleprotection (IEEE C37.94), GPS+NavIC time-synchronisation, ransomware-resilient NAS/SAN data-storage and cybersecurity equipment, selling primarily to Indian and international power utilities, defence agencies and critical-infrastructure customers across 110+ countries.

---

## The 7 Growth Triggers

### Trigger 1: Tejas Networks / GETCO marquee order (₹41.35 cr) — already executing **[HIGH]**
- **What:** Single largest order in company history from Tejas Networks (Tata Group; NSE: TEJASNET) for end-customer Gujarat Energy Transmission Corp (GETCO). A similar-sized 2nd GETCO project is L1.
- **Evidence:** *"Valiant has recently announced the receipt of its largest order of its corporate history from Tejas Networks Limited… The gross value of the purchase order is INR 4,135 Lacs… Valiant has also partnered with Tejas Networks, for 2nd project of GETCO. Wherein, Valiant with its business partner Tejas, are L1 bidders."* — PR_Feb25.pdf, p.5
- **Quantum:** ₹41.35 cr confirmed + ~₹40 cr 2nd GETCO project pending → potential ₹80 cr lifetime from this single relationship
- **Timeline:** FY26 / FY27 execution
- **Why HIGH:** PO received, end-customer named, public BSE intimation, partnership formalised. Money is on the way.

### Trigger 2: Gross order book at ₹86.94 cr — 4.8x quarterly run-rate **[HIGH]**
- **What:** Confirmed order book climbed from ₹63.6 cr (Feb-25) to ₹86.94 cr (Aug-25, end-Q1 FY26), against quarterly revenue of ₹18.12 cr.
- **Evidence:** *"Gross Order Book ₹8,694 [lacs] + Letter of Intent ₹450+ lacs + L1 Status (confirmed) ₹600+ lacs + Advance Stage Opportunities ₹2,000+ lacs."* — PR_Aug25.pdf, p.10
- **Quantum:** Implies **~₹75-80 cr revenue in next 4 quarters** vs FY25 revenue of ₹50.4 cr → **~50-60% growth visibility**
- **Timeline:** H2 FY26 + H1 FY27
- **Why HIGH:** These are signed POs and LoIs, not "opportunities." Conversion risk is execution, not award.

### Trigger 3: Operating leverage already visible in margins **[HIGH]**
- **What:** EBITDA margin trajectory **24.5% (FY24) → 31.1% (FY25) → 36.8% (Q1 FY26)**. As revenue base grows on essentially the same New Delhi plant + same SG&A cost block, incremental margins are exceptional.
- **Evidence:** AR25 ratio table p.79 — Operating profit margin 31.08% (FY25) vs 24.48% (FY24). PR_Aug25 p.9 shows Q1 FY26 std EBITDA margin 36.85%.
- **Quantum:** A further 200-300 bps of EBITDA margin on FY27 revenue of ~₹80 cr would add ₹1.6-2.4 cr of EBITDA on top of base growth.
- **Timeline:** FY26 progressively
- **Why HIGH:** Already empirically visible — not a forecast.

### Trigger 4: NAS/SAN Data Storage with Cyber-security (CII captive mandate) **[MEDIUM]**
- **What:** Indian government mandate that all Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) entities install captive data storage servers in India. VCL offers ransomware-resilient NAS/SAN with Quantum-Safe encryption, network isolation, data-diode and EMP-hardening — fully Made-in-India.
- **Evidence:** *"All Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) in India have been instructed to install captive data storage servers within India. Valiant solution advantages include Made in India, perpetual licence, and a pricing advantage. NAS and SAN Data storage servers will become high growth opportunities for our company, in the future."* — PR_Aug25.pdf, p.13. THDC (NTPC subsidiary) NAS order already executed (PR_May25.pdf, p.15-16).
- **Quantum:** Not disclosed, but a single THDC-style deployment is multi-crore; CII universe (banks, power, telecom, transport) is hundreds of entities.
- **Timeline:** FY26-FY28
- **Why MEDIUM:** Pilot deployments done (THDC); broader CII rollout pace is regulator-paced and unconfirmed.

### Trigger 5: PMU (Phasor Measurement Units) + Grid Islanding — global TAM **[MEDIUM]**
- **What:** PMU is core to "One Nation One Grid One Frequency" smart-grid build-out in India and globally. Grid Islanding solution actively marketed post Spain/Portugal April-25 blackouts. Globally only a handful of PMU manufacturers exist.
- **Evidence:** *"VCL is L1 in a bid for its Phasor Measurement Unit (PMU)… PMU is a key component of 'One Nation, One Grid, One Frequency' concept… Globally, there are very few foreign manufacturers, manufacturing PMUs at present."* — PR_Feb25.pdf, p.6. *"Valiant has received a confirmed Purchase Order from a PSU for its Phasor Measurement Unit (PMU)."* — PR_May25.pdf, p.15. *"Valiant's Grid Islanding solution… being actively marketed globally, and especially in light of the recent country-wide grid black-outs in Spain and Portugal."* — PR_Aug25.pdf, p.12.
- **Quantum:** PSU PMU order received; global TAM unbounded but conversion is project-cycle (3-5 yrs).
- **Timeline:** FY26-FY29
- **Why MEDIUM:** First PO booked, but no contracted pipeline for grid-islanding yet.

### Trigger 6: SEB pipeline of ₹80+ cr across 7 states **[MEDIUM]**
- **What:** Tender pipeline at State Electricity Boards of Chhattisgarh, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Assam, Kerala, Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra valued at ₹80+ cr (for Valiant's portion).
- **Evidence:** *"Valiant's forthcoming business opportunities for their products supplies at the State Electricity Boards (SEBs) of Chhattisgarh, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Assam, Kerala, Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra are valued at over ₹80+ crores (for Valiant)."* — PR_Aug25.pdf, p.11.
- **Quantum:** ₹80 cr "for Valiant" portion — even 50% conversion = ₹40 cr of incremental revenue.
- **Timeline:** FY26-FY27
- **Why MEDIUM:** Tender outcomes uncertain; pipeline has expanded (Feb-25 ₹75 cr → Aug-25 ₹80 cr) which is a positive signal but conversion rate not disclosed historically.

### Trigger 7: MPLS Routers with Encryption — defence & utility cybersecurity **[MEDIUM]**
- **What:** Encrypted MPLS routers for cyber-secure grid communications; defence interest building. Orders already received and executed for power utility networks.
- **Evidence:** *"VCL has received orders for its MPLS Routers with encryption for Power Utility applications. This is a significant development as this solution will provide cyber-secure communications for the grid communication network."* — PR_Aug25.pdf, p.12. *"Good growth potential is visualized in defence network applications, domestically and internationally."*
- **Quantum:** Not disclosed; MPLS router global TAM cited as $50bn → $100bn by 2027 (mgmt cites Mordor Intelligence) — small share of relevant addressable market is meaningful.
- **Timeline:** FY26 utility, FY27+ defence.
- **Why MEDIUM:** First orders booked; defence pipeline still aspirational.

### OPTIONALITY: Export market re-opening (South America, Australia, Philippines)
- **What:** Aug-25 update lists 16+ international reference customers (Ameren USA, Amentum, Department of National Defence Canada, Latvenergo, Zoox/Amazon, US Government Classified, Jotron Norway, Vietnam Ha Giang Power, etc.). New geographies in active bid: Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Peru, Australia, Philippines.
- **Evidence:** *"Orders from South America from Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Peru are likely to be received in the ongoing quarter…"* PR_Aug25.pdf, p.11.
- **Why OPTIONALITY:** Export revenue actually fell in FY25 (₹12.90 → ₹9.91 cr); recovery is aspirational rather than booked.

### OPTIONALITY: NSCS "Trusted Source" status
- Designated by National Security Council Secretariat as a Trusted Source for telecom-network products. Enables eligibility for Indian government captive telecom equipment procurement — a closed-tender universe potentially worth thousands of crores. No specific orders linked yet.

## What can break the thesis (top 3 risks)

1. **Working-capital reversal fails.** Receivables ballooned to 141 days (FY25 std). Management says debtors realised post-31-Mar-25 but does not publish quarterly balance sheets. If H1 FY26 balance sheet (Nov-25) still shows >120 days, the cash quality of the FY25 P&L becomes suspect and the stock is re-rated down hard. (Source: AR25 ratio table p.79.)
2. **Customer-concentration on Tejas / PGCIL / a handful of SEBs.** The Tejas-GETCO order alone equals ~80% of FY25 revenue. Any delay, dispute, or scope-reduction on this one contract is a quarterly miss. VCL has 110-country distributor reach but most revenue is from a small set of large tenders.
3. **Promoter dilution or governance event.** Promoters hold only 41.83% (relatively low for an Indian SME). One private placement of ~5.6% dilution already in FY24. Full independent-director re-constitution in Sep-2024. A repeat dilution at higher prices would cap upside; any unflagged related-party event would crush the multiple.

---

## Verdict

The HIGH-conviction triggers alone (Tejas/GETCO order in hand, ₹87 cr gross order book, margins already running at 37% in Q1 FY26) imply **revenue growth of 50-60% over the next 18 months with EBITDA growing faster** — that suffices to justify owning VCL even before crediting any of the medium-conviction stories (NAS, PMU, SEB pipeline, MPLS Routers). The MEDIUM triggers (CII captive storage, PMU global TAM, MPLS-encryption for grid/defence) collectively form a credible **multi-year compounding story**, and the OPTIONALITY items (NSCS Trusted Source, export breakthroughs) are free call options. The **single break-thesis risk is the working-capital normalisation claim** — if Nov-25 H1 FY26 results validate it, VCL goes from WATCH to BUY-WORTHY at any price under ~50x trailing P/E; if not, the position should not be taken.
