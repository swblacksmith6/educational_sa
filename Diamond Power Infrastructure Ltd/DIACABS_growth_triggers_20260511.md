# Diamond Power Infrastructure (DIACABS) — Growth Triggers 1-Pager
*As of 11 May 2026 | CMP: ~₹158 | Mcap: ~₹8,320 cr | Source: Screener.in / NSE / Co. filings*

## Business in one line
India's oldest single-location cables & conductors manufacturer (Vadodara, 110 acres), reset under NCLT in 2022 by GSEC Ltd + Rakesh Shah (Monarch Group), now executing a ₹3,500 cr+ Adani-anchored order book with backward integration into aluminium rod mills.

## The 6 Growth Triggers

### Trigger 1: Adani Energy Solutions AL-59 conductor contract — ₹1,349.11 cr [HIGH]
- **What:** LoI in Jul 2025 for supply of 24,080 km of AL-59 high-performance conductors for Jamnagar, Khavda-IV D HVDC, Navinal-II and Mahan-II transmission projects.
- **Evidence:** Co. disclosure to BSE/NSE 28-Jul-2025; reiterated in AR 2024-25, p. 38; rating reports.
- **Quantum:** ₹1,349 cr — ~80% of FY25 revenue locked in. Executed on a "km-rate + Price Variation" basis (margin protected).
- **Timeline:** Execution by **June 2028** — i.e., roughly ₹450 cr/year of incremental conductor revenue across FY26-FY28.

### Trigger 2: AL-59 backward integration via four rod mills (DICABS NexGen) [HIGH]
- **What:** Wholly-owned subsidiary DICABS NexGen Special Alloys Pvt Ltd setting up 4 in-house aluminium / AL-59 / HTSL rod mills at Vadodara. Capacity build-out: Mill 1 (Feb 2024) → Mill 2 (Mar 2025) → Mill 3 (Aug 2025) → Mill 4 (Oct 2025). Total target: ~75,000-1,00,000 MTPA wire-rod capacity.
- **Evidence:** Aug-2025 investor presentation slides 16-17; AR 2024-25 p. 38-40; multiple press releases on mill commissioning.
- **Quantum:** Each mill ~₹55 cr capex; ~₹220 cr cumulative. Internal supply removes ~₹3-5/kg trader margin paid earlier → ~150-200 bps gross-margin uplift at full utilisation.
- **Timeline:** All 4 mills operational by Oct 2025; full-utilisation revenue benefit visible from H2 FY26 onwards (already showing in Q3 FY26 gross margin jump from 14.3% to 23.3% YoY).

### Trigger 3: EHV cable capacity (220 kV+) commissioning [HIGH]
- **What:** India's first 700 kV / 28000 kVA UHV type-test lab is in-house; EHV line for 66 kV – 400 kV cables operational; 5th CCV line under commissioning by Mar 2026. Type-tested by CPRI, ERDA, KEMA, SABS.
- **Evidence:** Mar-2026 investor presentation slides 11-14; AR 2024-25 p. 39; type-test reports for 66 kV / 132 kV / 220 kV displayed.
- **Quantum:** EHV cables earn 25-30% gross margins (~2x of LT cables). At target 2,000 km/year × ₹4 cr/km value → up to ₹8,000 cr **maximum revenue capacity** (theoretical at full utilisation). Realistic FY28 capture: ~₹400-600 cr.
- **Timeline:** 5th CCV line by Mar 2026; meaningful EHV revenue from FY27.

### Trigger 4: Diversified Adani-Group order pipeline (cables) [HIGH]
- **What:** Multiple LoIs from Adani Group entities across cables (in addition to the AL-59 conductor order in Trigger 1).
- **Evidence:** Adani Green Energy ₹747.64 cr cable supply order; Adani Electricity Mumbai ₹100.54 cr HT cables (Apr 2026 – Feb 2027) + ₹45.47 cr (May 2026 – May 2027); Adani Energy Solutions ₹276 cr Khavda order (Nov 2025); Adani Green ₹19.57 cr (Feb 2026); Adani Wilmar, Adani Infra, Adani Total Gas as existing customers.
- **Quantum:** ~₹1,200-1,300 cr of cable orders from Adani Group entities (excl. conductor order). Combined with conductor order, total Adani exposure in order book ≈ ₹2,500 cr / ~70% of disclosed order book.
- **Timeline:** Execution spread over FY26-FY28.

### Trigger 5: ₹1,000 cr QIP — balance-sheet repair [MEDIUM]
- **What:** Board approved raising up to ₹1,000 cr via Qualified Institutions Placement; postal-ballot approval sought from shareholders.
- **Evidence:** Co. disclosure to exchanges; Aug-2025 and Mar-2026 investor presentations.
- **Quantum:** ₹1,000 cr would substantially repair the negative tangible net worth of ₹(881) cr — potentially flipping the company to positive book value. It would also fund the next leg of capex (transmission tower facility 48,000 TPA, copper cables facility under commissioning, retail brand investments).
- **Timeline:** Postal-ballot done in CY25; pricing and placement TBD — likely FY26-FY27. **Conviction is MEDIUM because pricing and marquee-investor demand are unknown.**

### Trigger 6: Retail brand launch (DICABS Urja / URJAFLEX / DICABS Renew) + CREDAI tie-up [OPTIONALITY]
- **What:** Three new B2C / B2B2C cable brands targeting residential, industrial and renewable energy segments. National Cable Partner status with CREDAI India 2025-26.
- **Evidence:** Mar-2026 investor presentation slides 36, 38; promoter perspective in AR 2024-25 p. 6-9.
- **Quantum:** No specific revenue guidance disclosed. Industry data: organised retail cables segment trades at ~30% gross margin vs <20% in institutional. Polycab earns ~40% of revenue from FMEG / B2C. Realistic 5-year potential: ₹500-1,000 cr of retail revenue.
- **Timeline:** Brand investments and distribution build-out underway; revenue contribution material from FY28+.

## What can break the thesis (top 3 risks)

1. **Adani Group decides to make cables/conductors in-house** (Adani has publicly announced cable manufacturing ambitions in 2026). The ₹2,500 cr Adani-linked book would migrate captive in 2-3 years.
2. **QIP fails or prices weakly**, leaving negative-net-worth unresolved and capping institutional ownership. Without an investment-grade credit rating, mutual-fund and FII ownership is structurally constrained.
3. **Raw-material (copper / aluminium) volatility outruns Price-Variation clauses.** The PV formula protects margins in conductors (sold km-rate × LME index) but is weaker in LT/HT cables sold to private buyers. A copper spike >20% in one quarter compresses cable margins by 200-300 bps.

## Verdict

The thesis carries 4 HIGH-conviction triggers (Adani AL-59 order, rod mills, EHV capacity, broader Adani book) which together justify ~₹3,500 cr of FY26-FY28 revenue at improving margins. The ₹1,000 cr QIP (MEDIUM) is the lever that fixes the only fundamental weakness — negative net worth — and unlocks institutional re-rating. Retail brands are an OPTIONALITY layer.

Sum of HIGH triggers easily justifies the operational thesis. **However, valuation has already priced in significant amount of this execution** (~64× annualised PAT, ~45× FY28E PAT) — leaving limited margin of safety. The thesis works if (a) Brickwork rating goes from "D / Issuer Not Cooperating" to BBB+/A-, (b) QIP closes at ≥₹200, and (c) FY28 revenue clears ₹4,000 cr at >13% EBITDA. Each of these is plausible but not yet evidenced.
