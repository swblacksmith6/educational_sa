## Scenario assumptions

Each scenario projects to **FY28** (the year by which the AESL ₹1,349 cr order finishes and the full capex is on-stream), then derives a target Mcap and per-share price.

### Bull case
- **Macro:** Cable & conductor industry sustains 14-15% growth; capex cycle continues; Adani / Birla entry does NOT cannibalise Diamond's order book (Adani group keeps Diamond as preferred non-captive vendor).
- **Execution:** All four rod mills run >90%; 5th CCV line + 10 MV cable lines fully utilised; EHV cables contribute ₹500-600 cr; exports clear 10% mix; QIP closes at ≥₹200, equity flips positive; Brickwork rating upgraded to BBB+/A-, mutual-fund ownership opens up.
- **FY28E revenue:** ₹4,500 cr (~2.8x of 9M FY26 annualised; CAGR ~40% FY26-FY28).
- **EBITDA margin:** 15% (Q3 FY26 already 14.7%; with NexGen contribution and EHV mix).
- **EBITDA:** ₹675 cr; depreciation ₹35 cr; interest ₹50 cr (debt down post-QIP); PBT ₹590 cr; tax 25% → **PAT ₹440 cr**.
- **Target P/E:** 50× (premium to Polycab on growth, marginally below KEI).
- **Target Mcap:** **₹22,000 cr** post-QIP (62.5 cr shares assuming QIP at ₹200 = 5 cr new shares).
- **Target price:** **₹350** per share.
- **Upside from CMP ₹158:** ~+120% over 24-30 months.

### Base case
- **Macro:** Industry grows 13-14%; Adani/Birla entry partly compresses pricing on industrial cables (-100 bps) but utility conductors remain Diamond-friendly.
- **Execution:** Rod mills 70-80% utilised; EHV contributes ₹300-400 cr; exports 6-7% of mix; QIP closes at ₹150-180 (modest dilution); credit rating moves to "BB+ / BBB-" — not investment grade but no longer "D / non-cooperating".
- **FY28E revenue:** ₹3,500 cr (2x of 9M FY26 annualised; CAGR ~30% FY26-FY28). This is roughly what the disclosed order book implies if 100% executed by FY28 plus modest new orders.
- **EBITDA margin:** 13% (200 bps better than FY25 due to NexGen + EHV; below bull case).
- **EBITDA:** ₹455 cr; D&A ₹35 cr; interest ₹70 cr; PBT ₹350 cr; tax 25% → **PAT ₹260 cr**.
- **Target P/E:** 38× (between RR Kabel and Polycab; small-cap discount for governance overhang).
- **Target Mcap:** **₹9,900 cr** (60 cr shares post-QIP at ₹165).
- **Target price:** **₹165** per share.
- **Outcome from CMP ₹158:** ~+5% over 24 months — essentially **fair-value at CMP**.

### Bear case
- **Macro:** Industry growth slows to 10-11%; Adani Group decides to in-source cables and conductors from FY28 (publicly hinted in 2026 announcements); cheap Chinese imports return as China rebalances away from EV.
- **Execution:** Adani-linked orders shrink from ₹2,500 cr to ₹1,200 cr over FY27-FY28; receivables stretch to 90+ days; QIP closes at ₹120 or below (deeper dilution); credit-rating remains "D / non-cooperating", limiting institutional float; raw-material spike compresses cable margins.
- **FY28E revenue:** ₹2,200 cr (only 1.4x of 9M FY26 annualised; reflects partial Adani migration).
- **EBITDA margin:** 9% (200 bps compression from base; competitive pressure).
- **EBITDA:** ₹198 cr; D&A ₹35 cr; interest ₹85 cr; PBT ₹78 cr; tax 25% → **PAT ₹58 cr**.
- **Target P/E:** 25× (sub-peer due to governance + concentration overhang).
- **Target Mcap:** **₹1,450 cr** (62 cr shares post-QIP at ~₹125).
- **Target price:** **₹23** per share.
- **Downside from CMP ₹158:** ~−85% over 24-30 months.

## Key catalysts to watch (next 12-18 months)

1. **QIP pricing and anchor book** (FY26-FY27) — if priced ≥₹200 with marquee anchors (SBI MF, ICICI Pru, FII), thesis re-rates to base/bull.
2. **Credit rating upgrade** — first step is appointment of a new agency (CRISIL / ICRA / India Ratings) and a "BB"/"BBB" rating. This unlocks debt mutual funds and lowers cost of borrowing.
3. **Q4 FY26 / FY26 full year results** (May 2026) — confirms ₹1,700-1,800 cr revenue at 12%+ EBITDA margin.
4. **MPS compliance** — promoter holding must drop from 84% to ≤75% by court-mandated deadline; expect OFS or fresh issuance.
5. **Adani Group cable manufacturing announcement** — any concrete capex plan by Adani Cables would be a thesis-breaker.
6. **AESL ₹1,349 cr order — execution pace** — first 30% should be visible in H1 FY27 revenue.
7. **Auditor / governance signals** — if Big 4 are appointed (joint statutory audit), big positive for re-rating.
