## Step 5 — Bear Case (5 bullets)

1. **Negative tangible net worth (-₹881 cr).** Equity capital base is genuinely impaired. Book value per share is meaningfully negative. The company is solvent only because of the 30-year, 0.001%-coupon ₹1,899 cr bond which the resolution applicant's SPV (Gomex Aviation) has bought back from the banks — i.e., the promoter group is effectively underwriting the senior creditors.
2. **"Issuer Not Cooperating" credit rating (BWR D).** Brickwork has been forced to reaffirm and withdraw the bank-loan rating because management has not cooperated with the rating agency since 2022. For a public listed company with ₹2,800 cr of rated debt, this is a serious governance signal — even if business performance has improved.
3. **Customer concentration on Adani Group.** Of the ₹3,500 cr+ order book, the Adani Group alone accounts for ~₹2,500 cr (AESL ₹1,349 cr + Adani Green ₹747 cr + Adani Electricity Mumbai ₹146 cr + Khavda ₹276 cr). Any pause or renegotiation by a single group could blow a hole in the FY27/FY28 thesis.
4. **Adani and Aditya Birla / UltraTech entering cables in 2026.** Both have signalled large in-house cable mfg ambitions. They could (a) take captive what is today Diamond's Adani-group revenue, and (b) compress the industry's margin pool. Diamond is small (~₹1,600 cr revenue annualized) vs Polycab ~₹29,000 cr and KEI / RR Kabel; pricing power is limited.
5. **Valuation already discounts the turnaround.** At ₹158 / ₹8,320 cr mcap, the stock trades at ~64× annualized 9M FY26 EPS and ~45× FY27E PAT — peer Polycab is ~45× with 5× the revenue, a 30% market share and clean balance sheet. The QIP of up to ₹1,000 cr will dilute the EPS further if executed at sub-₹200 prices.

## Step 6 — Verdict

**WATCH (revisit on trigger).** Diamond Power has unambiguously turned around — Q3 FY26 ran at a 14.7% EBITDA margin, the company is now profitable at scale (₹49.7 cr PAT in one quarter), the order book gives 2-year revenue visibility, and the four rod mills + EHV / MV capacity expansion clearly target the right end of the market.

That said, three issues keep this in the WATCH bucket rather than BUY-WORTHY: (1) the credit rating remains BWR D / non-cooperating despite the operational recovery — until the company gets re-rated by a credible agency, institutional re-rating is capped; (2) the valuation already prices in 2-3 years of execution at peer multiples that took Polycab a decade and a 30% market share to earn; and (3) the entry of Adani and Aditya Birla into the cable industry is a structural overhang that will be visible only in FY27.

A clean re-rating (Brickwork or CRISIL upgrade to BBB+/A-), QIP closure with marquee anchors, and consistent four-quarter execution at ≥12% EBITDA margin would convert this to a BUY. Until then, watch.
